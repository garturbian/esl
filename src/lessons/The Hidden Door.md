This is a story about a 10 year old boy named Max who discovers a hidden door in the woods behind his house. Max was a curious boy. He lived in a house with a big garden. Behind the garden, there was a forest. Max loved playing in the forest. One day, he decided to explore the woods behind his house. He walked for a while and found a small, old door. The door was hidden behind a tree. Max was surprised. He had never seen the door before.

1: Who is the main character in the story?  
2: How old is Max?  
3: What kind of boy is Max?  
4: Where does Max live?  
5: What is behind Max’s garden?  
6: Where does Max like to play?  
7: What did Max decide to do one day?  
8: What did Max find in the woods?  
9: Where was the door hidden?  
10: How did Max feel when he saw the door?

{% audioPlayer "/audio/the-hidden-door_1.mp3" %}

Max opened the door and found a small room. The room was dark and quiet. There was a small table in the room. On the table, there was a note. The note said: "For the curious and brave". Max was curious and brave, so he read on. The note told him to come back the next day with a flashlight.

1: What did Max find when he opened the door?  
2: What was the room like?  
3: What could Max see in the room?  
4: Where was the note found?  
5: What did the note say?  
6: What kind of person was Max, according to the note?  
7: What did Max do with the note?  
8: What did the note ask Max to do next?  
9: When should Max come back?  
10: What tool did Max need to bring with him?

{% audioPlayer "/audio/the-hidden-door_2.mp3" %}

Max was excited. He went back home and told his parents about the door and the note. His parents were surprised too. They didn't know about the door or the room. Max couldn't wait to go back to the room the next day.

1: Why was Max excited?  
2: Where did Max go after finding the door?  
3: Who did Max tell about the door and the note?  
4: How did Max’s parents feel when they heard the story?  
5: Did Max’s parents know about the door before?  
6: What did Max’s parents think about the room?  
7: Why was Max surprised about his parents’ answer?  
8: What could Max not wait to do?  
9: When did Max want to go back to the room?  
10: How do you think Max felt at the end of this part of the story?

{% audioPlayer "/audio/the-hidden-door_3.mp3" %}

The next day, Max took a flashlight and went back to the room. He opened the door and turned on the flashlight. The room was full of old books and strange objects. Max saw a telescope, a microscope, and a lot of other interesting things. He felt like he had discovered a treasure.

1: What did Max take with him the next day?  
2: Where did Max go again?  
3: What did Max do before entering the room?  
4: What did Max use to see inside the room?  
5: What was in the room?  
6: Name three objects Max saw in the room.  
7: How did Max feel when he saw the room?  
8: Why did Max think the room was special?  
9: What kind of things were in the room?  
10: What do you think Max will do next after this discovery?
