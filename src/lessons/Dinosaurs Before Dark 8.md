Jack jumped behind the magnolia tree. His heart was beating so fast he could hardly think. He peeked out at the giant monster. The horrible-looking creature was opening and closing his huge jaws.

1: Where did Jack jump?  
2: Why did Jack hide behind the tree?  
3: How was Jack’s heart beating?  
4: What could Jack hardly do because he was scared?  
5: What did Jack do to look at the monster?  
6: What did Jack see when he peeked out?  
7: What was the monster doing with its jaws?  
8: How did the creature look?  
9: Was Jack calm or afraid? Why do you think so?  
10: What do you think Jack will do next?

{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-ch-8_1.mp3" %}

His teeth were as big as steak knives. Don't panic. Think. Jack peered down at the valley. Good. The duck-billed dinosaurs were sticking close to their nests. Jack looked back at the Tyrannosaurus. Good. The monster still didn't seem to know he was there. Don't panic.

1: What were the monster’s teeth like?  
2: What does “Don't panic” mean in this situation?  
3: What did Jack tell himself to do?  
4: Where did Jack look down to?  
5: What were the duck-billed dinosaurs doing?  
6: Why was it good that they stayed near their nests?  
7: What did Jack look at after the valley?  
8: Did the Tyrannosaurus see Jack?  
9: Why was Jack thinking carefully?  
10: What danger was still near Jack?

{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-ch-8_2.mp3" %}

Think. Think. Maybe there's information in the book. Jack opened the dinosaur book. He found Tyrannosaurus rex. He read: Tyrannosaurus rex was the largest meat-eating land animal of all time. If it were alive today, it would eat a human in one bite. Great. The book was no help at all.

1: What did Jack try to do to help himself?  
2: What did Jack open?  
3: Which dinosaur did Jack find in the book?  
4: What does the book say about Tyrannosaurus rex size?  
5: What does the book say it would do to a human?  
6: Was the information helpful for Jack?  
7: How did Jack feel after reading the book?  
8: Why do you think the book was not helpful?  
9: What does “Think. Think.” show about Jack’s mind?  
10: What problem is Jack trying to solve?

{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-ch-8_3.mp3" %}

Okay. He couldn't hide on the other side of the hill. The Anatosauruses might stampede. Okay. He couldn't run to the tree house. The Tyrannosaurus might run faster. Okay. Maybe he should just wait. Wait for the monster to leave. Jack peeked around the tree. The Tyrannosaurus had wandered closer to the hill.

1: What place could Jack not hide in?  
2: Why was the other side of the hill dangerous?  
3: What might the Anatosauruses do?  
4: Where could Jack not run to?  
5: Why was the tree house not safe?  
6: What idea did Jack have at the end?  
7: What was Jack waiting for?  
8: What did Jack do while thinking?  
9: Had the Tyrannosaurus moved closer or farther away?  
10: How did this situation make Jack feel?

Something caught Jack's eye. Annie was coming down the rope ladder! Was she nuts? What was she doing? Jack watched Annie hop off the ladder. She went straight to the Pteranodon. She was talking to him. She was flapping her arms. She pointed at Jack, at the sky, at the tree house.

1: Who came down the rope ladder?  
2: What caught Jack’s eye?  
3: How did Annie come down?  
4: Where did Annie go first?  
5: What was Annie doing with her arms?  
6: Who did she talk to?  
7: What did she point at?  
8: Why did Jack think Annie was “nuts”?  
9: Do you think Annie is helping or making things worse?  
10: What might the Pteranodon think?

{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-ch-8_5.mp3" %}

She was nuts! "Go! Go back up the tree!" Jack whispered. "Go!" Suddenly Jack heard a roar. The Tyrannosaurus rex was looking in his direction. Jack hit the ground. The Tyrannosaurus rex was coming toward the hill.

1: What did Jack say about Annie?  
2: What did Jack whisper?  
3: What sound did Jack hear?  
4: What was the Tyrannosaurus doing?  
5: What did Jack do when he heard the roar?  
6: Why did Jack hit the ground?  
7: Where was the Tyrannosaurus moving?  
8: Was the danger getting closer or farther?  
9: How do you think Jack feels now?  
10: What might happen next?

Jack felt the ground shaking. Should he run? Crawl back into Dinosaur Valley? Climb the magnolia tree? Just then a giant shadow covered Jack. He looked up. The Pteranodon was gliding overhead. The giant creature sailed down toward the top of the hill. He was coming straight for Jack.

1: What did Jack feel under him?  
2: What question did Jack ask himself?  
3: What three choices did Jack think about?  
4: What covered Jack?  
5: What did Jack look up at?  
6: What was the Pteranodon doing in the sky?  
7: Where was the Pteranodon going?  
8: Who was it coming toward?  
9: Why is this moment very dangerous?  
10: What do you think will happen when it lands?
