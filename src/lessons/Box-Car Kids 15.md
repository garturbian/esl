"They will never go with you," Mrs. McAllister finally told the worried grandfather. "Unless we give you time to tell them the news slowly. Especially while Violet is so sick." "Could I see them?" the man begged. He sounded like a young boy. "I could pretend I am a friend of yours visiting you. I promise not to tell them who I am until you say it is okay."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch15_1.mp3" %}

"That might work," said Dr. McAllister. "If they start to like you before they know who you are, it will make things much easier." So, Mr. Cordyce’s driver went to get his expensive suitcase and his assistant. The cook, Irish Mary, was worried about cooking for such an important man, but the doctor told her she was a great cook and to just make a simple, delicious meal.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch15_2.mp3" %} 

At lunchtime, Mr. Cordyce met all the children except Violet. He smiled with joy to see Jess. Henry shook his hand, but he kept looking at the man carefully during the meal. Where have I seen him before? Henry wondered. Mrs. McAllister introduced them by their first names but forgot to say the man's last name. Jess thought, She forgot because she knows him so well. Even without a name, the stranger was very interesting.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch15_3.mp3" %}

He told them amazing stories about his life, his dog, and even a cucumber that grew inside a glass bottle. Henry was interested, and Benny was excited. "I would like to see that cucumber," Benny said. "Would you?" Mr. Cordyce asked with a smile. "Someday, if Mrs. McAllister agrees, you and I will go to my garden and pick it." "And we can bring it to Violet?" Benny asked.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch15_4.mp3" %}

"We will bring it to Violet," Mr. Cordyce promised. After lunch, the man sat in a chair in the doctor’s office to rest. He closed his eyes and breathed deeply as if he were sleeping. Jess came into the room with ice water. When she saw the "sleeping" man, she tiptoed quietly. Then, she turned back and carefully put a cushion under his feet. It was so gentle that he almost opened his eye.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch15_5.mp3" %}

No, he thought, if she really hated me, she would never have done that. The children did not hate him at all. They liked him very much. When he was finally allowed to see Violet, he brought her a bunch of beautiful flowers. He patted her head and told her he was sorry she was sick. He was very kind, and they all loved him.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch15_6.mp3" %}

He told Violet stories about his garden and his gardener. "I would love to see him," Violet said. "How long are you going to stay here?" Benny asked. Everyone laughed because they wanted him to stay. "As long as they will let me," the man answered softly. Henry heard those words and thought hard. Where have I heard that voice before? He went outside to work in the garden so he could watch the man.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch15_7.mp3" %}

Suddenly, he remembered! When he won the race, the man who gave him the cup had said, "I like your spirit, my boy." It was the same man! Henry sat down to think. He realized the man was the one who gave him the trophy with the wings. Then, he asked the doctor what the man's name was. "James Cordyce," the doctor said, "from the Steel Mills." Henry was shocked.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch15_8.mp3" %}

He walked outside and sat on the steps. That kind man is my grandfather? It seemed impossible, but in his heart, he knew it was true. He realized that Mrs. McAllister had not said his name on purpose. Henry saw the man walking toward the garden. "It is now or never," Henry thought. "I have to know!" Henry ran to catch up to him.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch15_9.mp3" %}

When the man turned around, Henry asked, "Are you James Henry Cordyce of Greenfield?" "I am, my boy," the man said, looking at him closely. "Does that mean you know that I know that you are Henry James Cordyce?" "Yes," Henry said simply. The man’s eyes filled with tears. For the third time, Mr. Cordyce shook his grandson's hand.
