Henry carried many bags in his arms and pockets. He said he would not open them or tell them about his day until dinner was ready. "Jess, you are amazing!" he said when he saw the new shelf and the clean dishes. They picked blueberries quickly, telling Henry all about the wonderful dump. Finally, they spread out their laundry-bag tablecloth, and Henry opened his packages.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch6_1.mp3" %}

"I bought more brown bread," he said, taking out the loaves, "and more milk from the same small store I visited yesterday. It is called a Delicatessen. The owner is a nice old man. I also bought some dried beef because we can eat it with our fingers. And I bought a big bone for the dog." "His name is Watch," Jess said. "All right," Henry agreed.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch6_2.mp3" %}

"I bought a bone for Watch." Watch ate the bone hungrily. It was a happy moment when Jess poured the cold milk into their cups and bowls. The children added plenty of blueberries and crumbled the bread into the milk. They ate with their spoons and were too happy to say a word for several minutes. Then, Henry told them about his day. "I earned a dollar this morning," he said proudly.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch6_3.mp3" %}

"I walked down a nice street with beautiful houses. There was a young man outside mowing his own lawn. He looked like a nice person." Henry stopped to eat a piece of beef. "He looked very hot," Henry continued. "Just as I got to his gate, his telephone rang. I heard it, so I called out to him and asked if he wanted me to finish mowing for him."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch6_4.mp3" %}

"And did he say yes?" Jess asked. "Yes! He said, 'Oh, please, yes!' " Henry smiled. "He wasn't used to doing it. I mowed the lawn and trimmed the edges. He said I did a better job than any boy he had ever hired. Then, he asked if I wanted a steady job." "Oh, Henry!" Violet and Jess cheered. "I told him yes. He said I can come back whenever I want, afternoon or tomorrow.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch6_5.mp3" %}

He didn't care." Henry finished his milk and looked happy. "It is a beautiful house with a big vegetable garden and a cherry orchard behind it. You should see the cherry trees! While I was trimming near the kitchen, the cook saw me. She was a kind, heavy woman." Henry laughed at the memory. "She asked me if I liked cookies. They smelled delicious, Benny! I told her I loved them.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch6_6.mp3" %}

She gave me one, and I put it in my pocket." "Did she see you?" Jess asked, worried. "No," Henry said confidently. "I pretended to chew for a long time." Benny looked at Henry's pocket, which was still a little bit full. "The doctor paid me a dollar, and the cook gave me this bag." Henry tossed the paper bag to Jess. Inside were twelve ginger cookies that smelled like cinnamon and sugar.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch6_7.mp3" %}

"I am going to write down everything I earn and spend," Henry said. "How will you write without a pencil?" Jess asked. "Violet has pieces of chalk in her sewing bag," Henry said. Violet got her bag and found the chalk. While the girls cleaned the dishes in the stream, Henry wrote his account on the wall of the freight car. He was very proud of it. "I Earned, $1.00. And we now have $3. 85 Cash on hand." Jess read aloud. He had written a list of what he bought: Milk: $. 24, Bread: $. 20, Cheese: $. 10, Beef: $. 20, Bone: $. 05, and Cloth: $. 10.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch6_8.mp3" %}

"Cloth?" Violet asked. "What is that for?" Henry laughed and handed her a package. "I thought we needed a tablecloth. It is not finished, though." Violet was very happy. She loved to sew. She started fixing the edges of the brown cloth with her needle. Henry left again with a light heart. When he returned that evening, he was excited. They had added many things to their home. The new broom stood in the corner. The tablecloth was washed and drying. Jess had washed Benny’s socks, and he was proud to wear them again.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch6_9.mp3" %}

Henry handed them a package. "Butter!" Jess cried. It was cool, sweet butter. They ate their supper of bread, butter, dried beef, and cookies. It felt like a royal meal. "These spoons are special," Henry explained. "If you turn them upside down and use the handle, they become knives." They used the handles to spread the butter. "I washed the doctor's car today," Henry said. "Tomorrow I will help in the garden.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch6_10.mp3" %}

I really want to go swimming in the brook!" "We could fix up a real swimming pool," Henry said. "We can do it," Violet said. "Jess and I know where there are big logs and flat stones." "We will do it soon!" Henry said. That night, Henry planned how to build the pool. He thought about using Benny’s wheels to make a cart to carry heavy stones.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch6_11.mp3" %}

Suddenly, he noticed Watch was not sleeping. He could see the dog's eyes shining in the dark. It was about eleven o'clock. Henry patted the dog, but Watch growled softly. "Shh!" Henry said. He sat up, and Jess sat up too. They heard nothing. "Close the door," Henry whispered. They pushed the heavy door shut until it was locked. They waited in silence. Watch continued to growl.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch6_12.mp3" %}

Finally, a branch cracked outside, and Watch barked loudly. Jess held the dog tightly so he would be quiet. "Someone knows we are here," Henry thought. He held the new broom in his hand, ready to protect his family. They waited for a long time, but nothing happened. Eventually, Watch stopped growling and lay down by Jess. Henry felt safe again. "It must have been a rabbit or something," he told Jess. The children went to sleep peacefully until morning.
