Amy is a happy girl. She is 10 years old. Yesterday was her birthday. She got a puppy! The puppy is small and cute. It is brown and white. Amy named the puppy Max. She loves Max very much. Max is very playful. He likes to run and jump. Amy plays with Max every day. She takes Max for a walk. Max is a good puppy. Amy is very happy with her new friend.

1. How old is Amy?  
2. What did Amy get for her birthday?  
3. What is the puppy's name?  
4. What color is the puppy?  
5. Does Amy like playing with the puppy?  
6. What does Max like to do?  
7. Does Amy take care of the puppy?
