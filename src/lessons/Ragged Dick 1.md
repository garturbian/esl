This story is about Ragged Dick. He is a poor 14-year-old boy who shines shoes on the streets of New York City. He wears old, torn clothes and has no home, so he sleeps in a wooden box. He is funny, friendly, and works hard to earn money. Dick is not a perfect boy, but he is honest. He refuses to steal, even when he has very little. His kindness and good personality make people like him.

1: Who is the main character in this story?  
2: How old is Ragged Dick?  
3: What job does Dick do?  
4: Where does Dick work?  
5: Why does Dick sleep in a wooden box?  
6: What kind of clothes does Dick wear?  
7: What three words describe Dick?  
8: Does Dick steal? Why or why not?  
9: Why do people like Dick?  
10: Do you think Dick is a good person? Why?

{% audioPlayer "/audio/rd-ch1_1.mp3" %}

"Wake up, Dick!" a porter said.  Dick opened his eyes. "What time is it?"  "Seven o'clock."  "Oh no! I'm late," Dick said. "I went to the theater last night."  "Where did you get the money?" the porter asked.  "I earned it by shining shoes," Dick said.  "Some boys steal money."  

1: Who told Dick to wake up?  
2: What did Dick ask when he opened his eyes?  
3: What time was it?  
4: Why was Dick late?  
5: Where did Dick go the night before?  
6: Who asked Dick about the money?  
7: How did Dick earn the money?  
8: What job does Dick do?  
9: What did the porter say some boys do?  
10: Do you think Dick earned his money honestly? Why?

{% audioPlayer "/audio/rd-ch1_2.mp3" %}

Dick shook his head. "Not me. I would never steal."  The porter smiled. "I'm glad to hear that."  Dick slept in a wooden box filled with straw. He jumped up, put on his old cap, and got ready for work. His clothes were torn and dirty, but he had a friendly face and an honest smile.

1: Did Dick steal money?  
2: What did Dick say about stealing?  
3: How did the porter feel after he heard Dick's answer?  
4: Where did Dick sleep?  
5: What was the wooden box filled with?  
6: What did Dick do after he got up?  
7: What did Dick put on before going to work?  
8: What were Dick's clothes like?  
9: What kind of face and smile did Dick have?  
10: What do these details tell you about Dick?

He carried his shoe-shine box to the street.  "Shine your shoes, sir?" he asked a gentleman.  "How much?"  "Ten cents."  As Dick worked, the gentleman laughed at his old clothes.  Dick laughed too. "This coat belonged to a famous general," he joked.  The gentleman smiled. "You are a funny boy."  

1: What did Dick carry to the street?  
2: Who did Dick speak to?  
3: What job did Dick offer to do?  
4: How much did Dick charge?  
5: Why did the gentleman laugh?  
6: How did Dick answer the gentleman?  
7: What did Dick say about his coat?  
8: Was Dick telling the truth about the coat?  
9: What did the gentleman call Dick?  
10: What does this conversation tell you about Dick's personality?

When the shoes were finished, the gentleman said, "I only have twenty-five cents. Can you bring me the change later?"  "Yes, sir," Dick answered.  "My office is on Fulton Street. My name is Greyson."  "I'll remember," Dick said.

2: How much money did the gentleman have?  
3: What did the gentleman ask Dick to do?

Mr. Greyson walked away and wondered, "Will that boy return the money?"  Dick was not a perfect boy. He sometimes wasted money on theater shows and cigars. But he had one very good quality: he was honest. He would not steal or cheat anyone.  That was why people liked Ragged Dick.
