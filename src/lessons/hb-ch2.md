The brakes on the car screamed. The driver of the car coming toward them suddenly turned the steering wheel hard. For a moment, it looked as if the wheels would not turn. Then the tires caught the gravel. The car swerved and shot past them. Sand and small stones flew around the two boys as they crouched beside their motorcycles at the edge of the high road.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_1.mp3" %}

The car had missed them by only a few inches! Frank saw the driver for a moment. The driver turned around as he passed. Even though the car was moving very fast and the road was dangerous, he shouted something at the boys. They could not understand his words. Then he shook his fist at them. The car was moving too fast for Frank to see the driver's face clearly.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_2.mp3" %}

But he saw that the man was not wearing a hat. He also saw that the man had thick red hair blowing in the wind. Then the car disappeared around the curve in front of them. It roared away in a cloud of dust. "That road hog!" Joe said when he had recovered from his surprise. "He must be crazy!" Frank said angrily. "He could have pushed both of us over the edge of the road!"

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_3.mp3" %}

"At the speed he was driving, I don't think he cared if he hit anyone." The two boys had every reason to be angry. The road was narrow and dangerous. Even a car moving at a normal speed was dangerous when it passed them. But the red-haired driver's wild driving was much worse. He had almost caused a serious accident. "If we ever catch him, I'm going to tell him exactly what I think!" said Frank.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_4.mp3" %}

"He almost ran us off the road, and then he had to shake his fist at us, too." "Road hog!" Joe said again. "A man like that should be in jail. If he only put his own life in danger, that would be bad enough. It's a good thing we were riding motorcycles. If we had been in another car, we would certainly have crashed." The boys continued their journey.
 
 {% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_5.mp3" %}

When they reached the curve ahead, they could see the village of Willowville below them. It lay in a small valley beside the bay. There was no sign of the dangerous driver. Frank delivered the legal papers that his father had given him. After that, the boys were free for the rest of the day. "It's too early to go back to Bayport," Frank said to Joe.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_6.mp3" %}

"Why don't we go and visit Chet Morton?" "Good idea," Joe agreed. "He has asked us many times to come and see him." Chet Morton was a school friend of the Hardy boys. His father worked as a real estate agent in Bayport, but the family lived in the country about a mile outside the city. Willowville was some distance away, but the boys knew a road that went across the country to the Morton family's home.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_7.mp3" %}

From there, they could return to Bayport. This would make their journey longer, but they would have the pleasure of visiting their friend. Chet was very popular with all the boys. One reason was that he owned a roadster. He drove it to school every day and often gave his friends rides after school. The Hardy boys rode along the country roads in the warm spring sunlight.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_8.mp3" %}

They enjoyed the freedom of their holiday, just as boys do when they have a day away from school. When they reached a small bridge over a ditch, not far from the Morton home, Frank suddenly stopped his motorcycle. He looked down into a group of bushes in the deep ditch. "Someone has had an accident," he said. An overturned automobile lay in the bushes. The car was completely destroyed.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_9.mp3" %}

It was upside down and looked like a pile of twisted metal. "The car must have been going very fast to get crushed like that," Joe said. "Maybe someone is still underneath it. Let's go and look." The boys left their motorcycles beside the road and went down to the wrecked car. But they saw no driver or passengers. "If anyone was hurt, someone has probably already taken them away.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_10.mp3" %}

This accident probably happened a day or two ago," Frank said. "Let's go. We can't do anything here." They left the wreck and returned to the road. Then they continued their journey. "At first, I thought it might be our red-haired speed demon," Frank said. "If it was him, he was very lucky to get out alive." The boys soon stopped thinking about the accident. Before long, they could see the Morton home.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_11.mp3" %}

It was a large, old farmhouse. It looked warm and comfortable. An apple orchard grew behind the house. As the boys rode down the lane, they saw someone waiting at the gate to the farmyard. "That's Chet," Frank said. "I'm glad he's home. I thought he might have gone out in his car." "That is strange," Joe agreed. "On a holiday like this, he usually doesn't stay around the farm."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_12.mp3" %}

As they came closer, Chet left the gate and walked down the lane to meet them. Chet was one of the most popular boys at Bayport High School. One reason was that he was always friendly and good-natured. He could find something funny in almost everything. He was always making jokes and having fun. He was rarely seen without a smile on his round, freckled face.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_13.mp3" %}

But today, the Hardy boys could see that something was wrong. Chet looked worried. As the boys stopped their motorcycles, they saw that their usually happy friend looked very unhappy. "What's the matter?" Frank asked as Chet hurried toward them. "You're just in time," Chet replied quickly. "You didn't meet someone driving my roadster, did you?" The two brothers looked at each other. They did not understand. "Your roadster?"

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_14.mp3" %}

"We would know it anywhere. No, we didn't see it," Joe said. "What happened?" "It's been stolen." "Stolen?" "An automobile thief stole it from the garage less than half an hour ago. He simply walked into the garage and drove away with the car. The hired man saw the roadster going down the lane, but he thought I was driving it. So he didn't think anything was wrong.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_15.mp3" %}

"Then, a little later, he saw me outside in the yard. He became suspicious, and we discovered that the roadster was gone." "Wasn't the car locked?" Frank asked. "That's the strange thing. The car was locked, even though the garage door was open. I don't understand how the thief got into it." "He must be a professional car thief," Frank said. "These thieves often carry many different keys with them.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_16.mp3" %}

But we are wasting time. We must chase him and tell the police. The hired man didn't see which way he went, did he?" "No." "There is only one road from here, and we didn't meet him. So he must have turned right at the end of the lane." "We'll chase him," said Joe. "Get on my motorcycle, Chet. We can still catch the thief." "Wait a minute!" Frank suddenly cried.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_17.mp3" %}

"I have an idea. Joe, do you remember the car we saw wrecked in the bushes?" "Of course." "Maybe the driver wrecked his own car. Then he stole the first car he could find." "What wreck?" Chet asked. The Hardy boys told him about the wrecked car they had found beside the road. Frank had just thought of something. Maybe the accident had happened only a short time earlier.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_18.mp3" %}

Perhaps the driver of the wrecked car had continued his journey in a stolen car. "That makes sense," said Chet. "Let's go and look at the wreck. We can find the license plate number. That might help us find the name of the car's owner." The three friends quickly started their motorcycles. They rode back along the road toward the place where they had seen the overturned car in the bushes.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_19.mp3" %}

They hurried because they knew that every second was important. The longer they waited, the farther the car thief could get. The wrecked car was still in the same place. No one had moved it. It seemed that no one had been near it since the boys had first found it. They left their motorcycles beside the road and went down into the bushes again to examine the wreck.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_20.mp3" %}

But they were disappointed. The car had no license plates. "That looks suspicious," said Frank. "It's more than suspicious," said Joe. He had moved a little to one side and was looking at the back of the automobile. "Frank, don't you remember seeing this car before? I didn't think of it until you mentioned the license plates." "I have been wondering if this is the same car that passed us on the road beside the shore," Frank said slowly.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_21.mp3" %}

"The car that passed us at the curve." "It is the same car. I'm sure of it. I noticed that it had no license plate because I wanted to remember its number. It was also a touring car of the same make as this one." "You're right, Joe. There is no mistake. The red-haired driver crashed into the ditch, just as we thought he might. Then he went to the nearest farmhouse.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_22.mp3" %}

That happened to be Chet's home. He stole the first car he found." "The wrecked car was the one driven by the man who almost pushed us over the edge of the road," Joe said. "And there is a very good chance that he is the man who stole Chet's roadster. He has red hair, too. At least we have those clues."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/hb-ch2_23.mp3" %}

"And he went past our farm instead of turning around and going back the way he came!" Chet cried. "Come on, fellows! Let's chase him! There wasn't much gas in the roadster anyway. Maybe he has already run out of gas." Excited about the chase, the boys climbed back onto the motorcycles. A few moments later, a cloud of dust rose from the road as the Hardy boys and Chet Morton began their fast chase after the red-haired thief.
