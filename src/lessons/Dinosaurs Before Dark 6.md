"Annie, look at this!" Jack called. "Look what I found!" Annie was on the hill. She was picking a flower from a magnolia tree. She did not listen. "Annie, look! A gold medallion!" Jack said. But Annie looked the other way. "Oh, wow!" she said. She saw something else on the hill. "Annie!" Jack shouted.

1: Who is calling Annie in the story and what does he want to show her?  
2: Where is Annie standing at the beginning, and what is she doing there?  
3: What is Annie focused on instead of listening to Jack?  
4: What important object does Jack find and try to show Annie?  
5: Does Annie pay attention to Jack when he calls her, and why or why not?  
6: What does Annie suddenly see on the hill that makes her react with surprise?


{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-chapter-6_1.mp3" %}

Annie ran down the hill. "Come back!" Jack called, but she was gone. Jack put the gold medallion in his pocket. Suddenly, he heard Annie scream. Then he heard a deep, loud sound like a horn. "Jack! Come here!" Annie called. Jack ran up the hill with his backpack.

1: What did Annie do after Jack called her to come back?  
2: Did Jack manage to stop Annie before she ran away?  
3: What did Jack do with the gold medallion after Annie left?  
4: What strange sound did Jack hear after Annie disappeared?  
5: Who called “Jack! Come here!” and why might she be calling him?  
6: What did Jack do after hearing Annie’s voice calling him?


{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-chapter-6_2.mp3" %}

At the top, he stopped in shock. The valley was full of nests made of mud. Inside were baby dinosaurs. Annie was near one nest. A large duck-billed dinosaur stood over her. It made a loud horn sound. "Stay calm. Don't move," Jack said. He walked slowly toward Annie. "Don't stand up. Crawl," Jack said.

1: What did Jack see when he reached the top of the hill?  
2: What was inside the nests in the valley?  
3: Where was Annie standing, and what was near her?  
4: What kind of dinosaur was standing over Annie, and what sound did it make?  
5: What advice did Jack give to Annie when he saw the danger?  
6: How did Jack move when he went toward Annie, and why did he move like that?


{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-chapter-6_3.mp3" %}

Annie crawled toward him. The dinosaur followed her. "Keep going," Jack said. Jack grabbed Annie's hand and pulled her close. "Stay low," he said. "Pretend to chew." "Chew?" Annie said. "Yes. Just do it," Jack said.

1: What did Annie do when Jack told her to move toward him?  
2: What did the dinosaur do while Annie was moving?  
3: What did Jack do when Annie reached him?  
4: What did Jack tell Annie to do after pulling her close?  
5: Did Annie understand Jack’s idea about “chewing” at first?  
6: Why do you think Jack told Annie to stay low and pretend to chew?

{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-chapter-6_4.mp3" %}

They both bent down and pretended to chew. The dinosaur became quiet. "I think she is calm now," Jack said. "Thanks, Jack," Annie said. "You must be careful," Jack said. "A mother is always near baby animals."

1: What did Jack and Annie do together to stay safe from the dinosaur?  
2: What changed in the dinosaur after Jack and Annie started pretending to chew?  
3: How did Jack feel about the situation after the dinosaur became quiet?  
4: What did Annie say to Jack after the danger seemed to pass?  
5: What warning did Jack give Annie about baby animals?  
6: Why do you think Jack told Annie to be more careful around baby dinosaurs?


{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-chapter-6_5.mp3" %}

Annie stood up again. She gave the dinosaur her magnolia flower. The dinosaur took it gently. Annie gave her more flowers. Then the dinosaur made a loud sound and became nervous. She ran away.

1: What did Annie do after she stood up again near the dinosaur?  
2: How did the dinosaur react when Annie gave her the magnolia flower?  
3: What else did Annie do after the dinosaur took the flower?  
4: How did the dinosaur’s behavior change after Annie gave her more flowers?  
5: What did the dinosaur do after she became nervous?  
6: Why do you think the dinosaur ran away suddenly?


{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-chapter-6_6.mp3" %}

Jack looked in his book. It said these dinosaurs live in groups. Some mothers stay with babies. Others find food. Suddenly, the dinosaur looked scared again. Annie said, "We were friends!" But Jack looked far away. He saw a huge monster coming. It had big legs, a long tail, and sharp teeth. Jack whispered, "Tyrannosaurus rex."

1: What did Jack read about how these dinosaurs live and take care of their babies?  
2: What two jobs do the mother dinosaurs do in their group?  
3: How did the dinosaur feel again after things seemed calm?  
4: What did Annie say about her relationship with the dinosaur?  
5: What did Jack see coming in the distance?  
6: What details did Jack notice about the monster’s body and face?
