In less than an hour, the whole town was talking about the news. The chauffeur told the maids, the maids told the grocery man, and soon everyone knew that James Cordyce had finally found his four grandchildren. Jess and Benny were walking across the lawn to pick flowers for Violet when they heard Henry say, "But, Grandfather..." "Grandfather!" Jess repeated, turning around in surprise. "Yes, Jess," Henry said happily.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch16_1.mp3" %}

"He is the man we have been running away from all this time." "I thought you were old," Benny said. "And very grumpy. Jess said so!" Jess turned pink. She felt silly for running away from such a kind man. But her grandfather just smiled. He patted her short hair and suggested they all go see Violet. Benny rushed into her room, pulling his grandfather by the hand and shouting, "It’s Grandfather, Violet!

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch16_2.mp3" %}

And he is nice after all!" When Violet understood who he was, she was very happy to rest against her pillows and listen. "Where have you been living?" Mr. Cordyce asked. The whole group, including Dr. McAllister and his mother, started to laugh. "You should see it!" Dr. McAllister said, wiping his eyes. "What do you mean?" the children asked. "You never saw it in the daytime!"

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch16_3.mp3" %}

"I have seen it many times in the daytime," the doctor teased. Mr. Cordyce was confused. "Seen what?" The children then explained everything—the pine-needle beds, the beautiful dishes they found, the freight car, the fireplace, and the swimming pool. "That is where Violet got her sickness," the doctor explained. "Sitting by the pool. I knew it from the start." "You knew?" Henry asked. "How did you know she sat there?"

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch16_4.mp3" %}

"I was your most frequent visitor," the doctor said, laughing. "The first night you mowed my lawn, I followed you to the hill to see where you lived because I was curious." "But you didn't see the freight car then, did you?" Jess asked. "No, but I came back that night and looked around," the doctor replied. "And when I heard the dog bark, I knew someone was there."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch16_5.mp3" %}

"You came back?" Jess asked, amazed. "Yes. Every time I knew you were safe in my garden, I visited the car to make sure you had enough food and dishes," the doctor said. "When I saw you had a strainer, a vase of flowers, and a glass bowl, I stopped worrying." "Didn't you know they were my children?" Mr. Cordyce asked. "Why didn't you call me?"

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch16_6.mp3" %}

"They were having such a good time," the doctor confessed. "I was curious to see how long they could take care of themselves. They are excellent business managers, Mr. Cordyce!" Mr. Cordyce listened carefully. "How did you know Violet sat by the pool?" Jess asked again. "I went up there twice when I knew Henry was at my barn," the doctor explained. "I hid behind the big white rock."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch16_7.mp3" %}

"That's Lookout Rock!" Jess said. "And I had a wonderful time when I visited with Mother," the doctor added. "Have you seen it, too?" the children cried. "I have!" Mrs. McAllister said. "I even drank from your well." "Everyone has seen it but me," Mr. Cordyce said patiently. "We will show you!" Benny shouted. "And I will show you my cart and my pink cup!"

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch16_8.mp3" %}

"When Violet gets well, we will all go up there," Mr. Cordyce promised. "If you show me your house, I will show you mine." "Do you have a house?" Benny asked, surprised. "Yes. You can live there with me if you like," Mr. Cordyce replied. "I have been looking for you for two months." With Mrs. McAllister’s care, Violet soon became strong again.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch16_9.mp3" %}

A few days later, they all drove to the freight-car house in a large car. When they arrived, the children were like wild things. They were so happy to be home! Watch the dog ran around, sniffing everything. Mr. Cordyce sat on a rock and watched them, smiling at Mrs. McAllister. Benny showed him the fireplace, the well, and the "refrigerator." They climbed into the freight car and looked at the beds and the shelf.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch16_10.mp3" %}

Each dish had a special story. They ate chicken sandwiches on their tablecloth, and Benny drank from his pink cup. When the sun began to set, they had to leave. "Tomorrow," Mr. Cordyce suggested as they drove home, "will you all come and see my house?" "Oh, yes!" the children agreed, happy to have found a new life with their grandfather.
