Jack crawled through a hole in the floor of the tree house. The tree house was full of books. There were books everywhere. Some books were very old with dusty covers. Some books were new with bright, shiny covers. "Look. You can see very far away," said Annie. She looked out of the window. Jack looked out too. He saw the tops of many trees below. In the distance, he saw Frog Creek library, the elementary school, and the park. Annie pointed the other way.

1: Where did Jack crawl through?  
2: Where was Jack inside?  
3: What was inside the tree house?  
4: Were there a few books or many books?  
5: What kind of covers did some old books have?  
6: What kind of covers did some new books have?  
7: Who said, “You can see very far away”?  
8: Where did Annie look?  
9: Where did Jack look?  
10: What did Jack see below the tree house?  
11: What did Jack see in the distance?  
12: Can you name the places Jack saw in the distance?  
13: What did Annie do with her hand?  
14: Which direction did Annie point?  
15: Why do you think Annie was excited?  
16: Have you ever seen a view from high up?

"Let me see," said Jack. He put down his bag and took the book. "You read that one. I will read the castle book," said Annie. "No, we should not," said Jack. "We do not know who the books belong to." But Jack still opened the dinosaur book. He could not stop himself. He saw a picture of a flying reptile called a Pteranodon. He touched its large wings. "Wow," he whispered. "I wish I could see one for real." He looked at the picture carefully.

Suddenly, Annie screamed. "Stop pretending, Annie," said Jack. "No, really!" Annie said. Jack looked out of the window. A giant flying creature was moving over the trees. It had a strange crest on its head, a long beak, and big wings. It was a real Pteranodon. The creature flew through the sky toward the tree house. It looked like a flying plane. The wind started to blow. The leaves shook.

1: What did Annie do suddenly?  
2: What did Jack say to Annie?  
3: Did Annie say she was pretending?

5: Where did Jack look?

The creature suddenly flew up high into the sky. Jack almost fell trying to see it. The wind got stronger and louder. The tree house began to spin. "What's happening?" shouted Jack. "Get down!" shouted Annie. She pulled him away from the window. The tree house spun faster and faster. Jack closed his eyes and held Annie. Then everything became still. Jack opened his eyes. Sunlight came through the window. Annie was there. The books were there. His bag was there. The tree house was still in an oak tree. But it was not the same oak tree.

2: Why did Jack almost fall?

8: What happened to the tree house as it spun?

13: What came through the window?
