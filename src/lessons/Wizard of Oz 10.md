It took some time for the Cowardly Lion to wake up. He had slept among the red flowers for a long while and breathed in their bad smell. But when he opened his eyes and rolled off the truck, he was very glad to be alive. "I ran as fast as I could," he said. He sat down and yawned. "The flowers were too strong for me."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_1.mp3" %}

"How did you get me out?" Then they told him about the field mice. They told him how the mice saved his life. The Cowardly Lion laughed and said, "I always thought I was very big and fierce. Yet tiny flowers almost killed me, and small mice saved my life. How strange that is! But friends, what shall we do now?" "We must walk until we find the yellow brick road again," said Dorothy.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_2.mp3" %}

"Then we can go straight to the Emerald City." The Lion felt rested and well again. They all started on their trip and liked the walk through the soft, green grass. Soon they found the yellow brick road. They turned toward the Emerald City where the Great Oz lived. The road was smooth and nice. The land around them was pretty. The travelers were happy to leave the dark forest and its many dangers far behind.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_3.mp3" %}

Once more, they saw fences by the road. These fences were painted green. They passed a small house where a farmer lived, and it was painted green, too. They walked past several houses that afternoon. Sometimes people came to the doors to look at them. The people looked like they wanted to ask questions. But no one came close or spoke to them because they were afraid of the big Lion.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_4.mp3" %}

All the people wore lovely green clothes and tall hats like the Munchkins. "This must be the Land of Oz," said Dorothy. "We must be getting close to the Emerald City." "Yes," said the Scarecrow. "Everything here is green. In the Munchkin land, blue was the favorite color. But these people do not look very friendly. I am afraid we cannot find a place to sleep tonight."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_5.mp3" %}

"I want something to eat besides fruit," said the girl. "I am sure Toto is very hungry, too. Let us stop at the next house and talk to the people." They came to a big farmhouse. Dorothy walked right up to the door and knocked. A woman opened the door just a little bit. She looked out and said, "What do you want, child? And why is that huge Lion with you?"

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_6.mp3" %}

"We want to stay here tonight, if you will let us," said Dorothy. "The Lion is my friend. He would not hurt you for anything." "Is he tame?" asked the woman. She opened the door a bit wider. "Oh, yes," said the girl. "He is a great coward, too. He is more afraid of you than you are of him." "Well," said the woman. She thought about it and looked at the Lion again.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_7.mp3" %}

"If that is true, you can come in. I will give you some food and a place to sleep." They all went inside the house. There were two children and a man, along with the woman. The man had a hurt leg. He lay on a sofa in the corner. They looked very surprised to see such strange visitors. While the woman set the table, the man asked, "Where are you all going?"

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_8.mp3" %}

"To the Emerald City," said Dorothy. "To see the Great Oz." "Oh!" said the man. "Are you sure Oz will see you?" "Why not?" she asked. "People say he never lets anyone see him. I have been to the Emerald City many times. It is a wonderful place. But no one has ever let me see the Great Oz. I do not know anyone who has seen him."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_9.mp3" %}

"Does he never go outside?" asked the Scarecrow. "Never. He sits day after day in the big room of his palace. Even the people who work for him do not see his face." "What does he look like?" asked the girl. "That is hard to say," said the man. He thought for a minute. "Oz is a great wizard. He can change his shape. Some people say he looks like a bird."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_10.mp3" %}

"Some say he looks like an elephant. Some say he looks like a cat. To other people, he looks like a fairy or a little man. No one knows what the real Oz looks like." "That is very strange," said Dorothy. "But we must try to see him. If we do not, our trip was for nothing." "Why do you want to see the terrible Oz?" asked the man.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_11.mp3" %}

"I want him to give me some brains," said the Scarecrow quickly. "Oh, Oz can do that easily," said the man. "He has more brains than he needs." "And I want him to give me a heart," said the Tin Man. "That is easy for him, too," the man said. "Oz has a big box of hearts in all shapes and sizes." "And I want him to give me courage," said the Cowardly Lion.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_12.mp3" %}

"Oz has a big pot of courage in his room," said the man. "He keeps a gold plate over it so it will not spill. He will gladly give you some." "And I want him to send me back to Kansas," said Dorothy. "Where is Kansas?" asked the man with surprise. "I do not know," said Dorothy sadly. "But it is my home. I know it is somewhere." "Maybe so."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_13.mp3" %}

Well, Oz can do anything. I think he can find Kansas for you. But first, you must get to see him. That will be hard. The Great Wizard does not like to see people. He always gets his own way. But what do you want?" he asked Toto. Toto only wagged his tail because he could not talk. The woman called out that supper was ready. They sat around the table.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_14.mp3" %}

Dorothy ate good porridge, scrambled eggs, and white bread. She enjoyed her meal. The Lion ate some porridge, but he did not like it. He said oats were food for horses, not lions. The Scarecrow and the Tin Man ate nothing. Toto ate a little bit of everything and was very happy to have a good meal. The woman gave Dorothy a bed. Toto lay down on the floor next to her.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_15.mp3" %}

The Lion watched the door so no one would bother her. The Scarecrow and the Tin Man stood quietly in the corner all night. They did not sleep because they did not need to. The next morning, the sun came up. They started walking again. Soon they saw a bright green light in the sky ahead. "That must be the Emerald City," said Dorothy. They walked on.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_16.mp3" %}

The green light grew brighter and brighter. At last, they were near the end of their trip. But it was afternoon before they reached the high wall around the city. The wall was tall, thick, and bright green. Right at the end of the yellow brick road was a big gate. It was covered in emeralds. They shone so brightly in the sun that even the Scarecrow's painted eyes hurt from the light.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_17.mp3" %}

There was a bell by the gate. Dorothy pushed the button and heard a soft ring inside. The big gate slowly opened. They all walked through into a tall room. The walls shone with thousands of emeralds. A little man stood there. He was about the same size as the Munchkins. He wore green clothes from head to toe, and even his skin had a green tint.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_18.mp3" %}

A big green box sat next to him. The man looked at Dorothy and her friends. He asked, "What do you want in the Emerald City?" "We came to see the Great Oz," said Dorothy. The man was so surprised that he sat down to think. "It has been many years since anyone asked to see Oz," he said. He shook his head. "He is very powerful."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_19.mp3" %}

"If you came to bother the wise wizard for no good reason, he might get mad and destroy you all at once." "This is not a foolish trip," said the Scarecrow. "It is very important. And people told us Oz is a good wizard." "He is," said the green man. "He rules the Emerald City well. But if people are not honest, or if they just want to look out of curiosity, he is very scary."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_20.mp3" %}

"Few people dare to ask to see his face. I am the Guardian of the Gates. Since you want to see the Great Oz, I must take you to his palace. But first, you must put on these glasses." "Why?" asked Dorothy. "If you do not wear glasses, the bright light of the Emerald City will blind you. Even the people who live here must wear glasses day and night.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_21.mp3" %}

Oz made this rule when the city was built, and I have the only key that opens them." He opened the big box. Dorothy saw that it was full of glasses in all sizes. Every pair had green glass lenses. The Guardian of the Gates found a pair that fit Dorothy and put them over her eyes. Two gold bands went around her head.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_22.mp3" %}

He locked them together with a small key on a chain around his neck. Once they were on, Dorothy could not take them off. But she did not want the bright light to blind her, so she said nothing. Then the green man put glasses on the Scarecrow, the Tin Man, the Lion, and even little Toto. He locked all of them with the key.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/woz-ch10_23.mp3" %}

The Guardian of the Gates put on his own green glasses. He told them he was ready to show them the way to the palace. He took a big gold key from the wall, opened another gate, and led them into the streets of the Emerald City.
