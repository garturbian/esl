“Help! A monster!” said Annie.
“Yeah, right,” said Jack. “A real monster in Frog Creek, Pennsylvania.”
“Run, Jack!” said Annie. She ran up the road.
Oh, no.
This was what happened when he spent time with his seven-year-old sister.
Annie loved pretending. But Jack was eight and a half. He liked real things.

1: Who shouted, “Help! A monster!”?  
2: What did Annie say she saw?  
3: Did Jack believe there was a real monster?  
4: Where did Jack say the monster was?  
5: What did Annie tell Jack to do?  
6: Where did Annie run?  
7: How old was Annie?  
8: Who was Annie’s brother?  
9: How old was Jack?  
10: Did Annie like pretending?  
11: What kind of things did Jack like?  
12: Who liked real things, Annie or Jack?  
13: Did Jack and Annie think the same way?  
14: Why do you think Jack did not believe Annie?  
15: Do you like pretending or do you like real things? Why?

“Be careful, Jack! The monster is coming! Race me!”

“Annie!” he shouted again.
“Jack! Jack! Come here!”
Jack sighed. “This better be something good,” he said.
Jack left the road and walked into the woods. The trees were shining in the golden afternoon light.
“Come here!” called Annie.
There she was, standing under a tall oak tree.
“Look,” she said.
She pointed to a rope ladder.
It was the longest rope ladder Jack had ever seen.

2: Who called Jack?  
3: What did Annie say to Jack?

5: What did Jack say?

7: Where did Jack go?  
8: What were the trees shining in?  
9: What color was the afternoon light?  
10: What did Annie tell Jack to do?  
11: Where was Annie standing?  
12: What kind of tree was Annie standing under?  
13: What did Annie say when Jack came?

“Wow,” he said quietly.
The ladder went all the way to the top of the tree.
At the top was a tree house. It was between two branches.
“That must be the highest tree house in the world,” said Annie.
“Who built it?” asked Jack. “I’ve never seen it before.”
“I don’t know. But I’m going up,” said Annie.
“No. We don’t know who owns it,” said Jack.
“Just for one little minute,” said Annie. She started climbing the ladder.
“Annie, come back!”

1: What did Jack say quietly?  
2: Where did the rope ladder go?  
3: What was at the top of the tree?  
4: Where was the tree house built?  
5: What did Annie think about the tree house?  
6: What question did Jack ask about the tree house?  
7: Had Jack seen the tree house before?  
8: Did Annie know who built the tree house?  
9: What did Annie decide to do?  
10: What did Jack say about going into the tree house?  
11: Did Jack think it was safe to go up?  
12: What did Annie ask Jack before climbing?  
13: What did Annie start doing?  
14: What did Jack say when Annie climbed?  
15: Why do you think Jack did not want Annie to go up?  
16: What would you do if you saw a tree house you had never seen before?

But she kept climbing.
Jack sighed. “Annie, it’s getting dark. We have to go home.”
Annie went inside the tree house.
“Annie!”
Jack waited for a moment. He was about to call again when Annie put her head out of the window.
“Books!” she shouted.
“What?”
“It’s full of books!”
Oh, wow! Jack loved books.
He fixed his glasses. He held the sides of the rope ladder and started climbing up.
