They Look Outside. Jack looks out the window. He sees many things outside: big green plants, a river, and mountains. He compares what he sees to his book picture. The world outside is exactly like the picture!

1. Where does Jack look?  
2. What does Jack see outside?  
3. Does Jack see a river?  
4. What color are the plants?  
5. Does Jack see mountains?  
6. What does Jack compare the world to?  
7. Is the picture in his book?  
8. Is the world outside different from the picture?  
9. How is the world outside like the picture?  
10. What three things does Jack see outside?  
11. Do you think Jack is surprised? Why?  
12. What would you like to see outside your window?

{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-chapter-3_1.mp3" %}

They See Dinosaurs A giant flying animal (a Pteranodon) flies in the sky. It lands near their tree house. They are very surprised! "Where are we?" Jack asks.

1. What do Jack and Annie see in the sky?   
2. Is the animal small or giant?  
3. What is the name of the flying animal?  
4. What does the Pteranodon do?  
5. Where does it land?  
6. Is it far from the tree house?  
7. How do Jack and Annie feel?  
8. Are they surprised?  
9. Who asks a question?  
10. What question does Jack ask?  
11. Why do you think Jack asks, "Where are we?"  
12. How would you feel if you saw a giant flying animal?  


{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-chapter-3_2.mp3" %}

They Talk About It Annie and Jack ask what happened. They remember they were reading about dinosaurs. The wind got loud, and the tree house started to spin! They landed in this strange place. Jack thinks: "This cannot be real." But Annie says: "No, it is real!"

1. Who talks about what happened?  
2. What do Annie and Jack remember?  
3. What were they reading about?  
4. What happened to the wind?  
5. Was the wind quiet or loud?  
6. What started to spin?  
7. Did the tree house stay still?  
8. Where did Annie and Jack land?  
9. Is the place normal or strange?  
10. What does Jack think?  
11. Does Jack think it is real?  
12. What does Annie say?  
13. Does Annie agree with Jack?  
14. Who thinks it is real, Jack or Annie?  
15. Why do you think Annie and Jack are confused?  
16. What would you think if a tree house started to spin?


{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-chapter-3_3.mp3" %}

The Big Secret (The Book) They look at the book again. Jack reads words under the picture. It says this creature lived very long ago—millions of years ago! This means they should not be here!

1. What do Jack and Annie look at again?  
2. Who reads the words under the picture?  
3. Where are the words?  
4. What do the words talk about?  
5. Did the creature live a long time ago?  
6. How long ago did it live?  
7. Did it live millions of years ago?  
8. Is millions of years ago a short time or a very long time?  
9. What does Jack learn from the book?  
10. Why is this a big secret?  
11. Should Jack and Annie be there?  
12. Why should they not be there?  
13. How do you think Jack feels after reading the words?  
14. How do you think Annie feels?  
15. What would you do if you were in a place from millions of years ago?


{% audioPlayer "/audio/dbd-chapter-3_4.mp3" %}

They Decide to Go Down Annie sees the flying dinosaur, and she is excited. She thinks it is "nice." Jack tries to stop her because he is scared. But Annie goes down the rope ladder. She walks toward the big animal.

1. Who sees the flying dinosaur?  
2. How does Annie feel?  
3. Is Annie excited or afraid?  
4. What does Annie think about the dinosaur?  
5. Who is scared?  
6. Why does Jack try to stop Annie?  
7. Does Annie listen to Jack?  
8. What does Annie do next?  
9. How does Annie go down from the tree house?  
10. Does she climb down the rope ladder?  
11. Where does Annie walk?  
12. Does she walk away from the animal or toward it?  
13. Is the animal big or small?  
14. How do you think Jack feels when Annie goes down?  
15. Would you walk toward a dinosaur? Why or why not?
