Life in the freight car was happy. Every day brought a new adventure. Mrs. McAllister found out that Violet was a good seamstress, so she sent her fine linen handkerchiefs to hem. Violet loved the work. She sat by the swimming pool every day to sew while Benny played with his toy boats. Their pantry was now full of treasures from the dump, including a real bread knife and a beautiful glass bowl.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch14_1.mp3" %}

Henry had thirty-one dollars in his savings account under the name "Henry James." He worked hard for Dr. McAllister, who had stopped asking him personal questions. Benny was learning to read, and the girls spent time making pillows for their beds. Every day, Violet picked flowers for the little vase. Life was good, but one day, Jess tried to teach Benny math.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch14_2.mp3" %}

She tried to show him that if you have two apples and take one away, you have one left. Benny didn't agree. "No," he said, "I still have two!" Jess was surprised. "What if Watch the dog took one?" she suggested. "Watch wouldn't take one," Benny said firmly. "Would you, doggie?" Watch just opened one eye and wagged his tail. Violet tried to help by using her chalk.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch14_3.mp3" %}

She wrote "two minus one equals" on the outside of the freight car. "If you have two things and somebody takes one away, you must have one left," she explained. Benny looked at the number 2 on the car. Then he covered the number 1 with his hand. "See?" he said. "If I take away the one, the two is still left right there!" Jess and Violet laughed so hard they almost cried.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch14_4.mp3" %}

But then, things turned serious. Violet couldn't stop crying. Jess realized her sister was truly sick. She helped Violet into the car, made her a soft bed of pine needles, and put cool cloths on her forehead. When Henry came home, he was very worried. Violet started to cough. "We can't take her to a hospital," Henry said. "If we go there, we have to give our names, and Grandfather will find us."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch14_5.mp3" %}

By ten o'clock that night, Violet was shivering and shaking. Henry ran as fast as he could to Dr. McAllister’s house. He was so scared he didn't even notice how fast he was running. The doctor drove his car to the hill, picked up the sick little girl, and carried her to his house. "I am afraid it is pneumonia," the doctor told his mother. They worked all night to keep Violet warm.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch14_6.mp3" %}

The next morning, the doctor told them it wasn't pneumonia, but Violet was still very ill. Around nine o'clock, a visitor came to see the doctor. It was Mr. Cordyce, the man who owned the steel mills. While he waited, Benny wandered into the room. "Where is the doctor?" the man asked. "He is upstairs," Benny said. "He is taking care of my sister Violet. She is sick."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch14_7.mp3" %}

Mr. Cordyce looked surprised. "I have lost my grandchildren," he said. "The doctor might know where they are. He would be about your age." Benny didn't realize he was talking to his own grandfather. "Well, if you don't find him, you can have me," Benny said kindly. "I like you." Mr. Cordyce was surprised, but he liked Benny. When the doctor finally came downstairs, he saw Benny sitting on the man's lap, looking at a picture in his watch.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch14_8.mp3" %}

"Go find Watch, Benny," the doctor said. Benny ran off. "I expected you, Mr. Cordyce," the doctor said. "I heard you were looking for your grandchildren." "Is it true?" Mr. Cordyce asked. "Where are they?" "They are here," the doctor said. "But they are terrified that you will find them." "Why?" Mr. Cordyce asked. "They have changed their name," the doctor explained. "The older boy calls himself 'Henry James'."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch14_9.mp3" %}

Mr. Cordyce turned red. "That boy?" he said. "The wonderful runner?" He finally realized that the children he had been looking for were the very same ones he had met at the race. Things were about to change very quickly.
