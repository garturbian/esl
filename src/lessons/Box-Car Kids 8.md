The next morning, the children woke up to the sound of church bells ringing in the valley. "I like the music," Violet said, "but I have to get to work." "It is a good day to wash our clothes," Jess added. "We will be playing in the stream all day anyway." After breakfast, Henry looked at the spot they chose for their swimming pool.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch8_1.mp3" %}

It was a hole about three yards wide, but it was very shallow. He measured it with a stick and then asked Violet to use the tape measure from her sewing bag. The water was only ten inches deep. Henry had a plan. "We need to drag some big logs across this narrow part and fill the gaps with stones and bushes," he said. "It should be three feet deep when we finish."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch8_2.mp3" %}

"But Benny might drown!" Jess said. They measured Benny and found he was forty-two inches tall. Since they wanted the pool to be three feet (thirty-six inches) deep, it would be safe for him. The older children dragged the heavy logs to the stream. Violet and Benny used the cart to bring stones. When Henry needed help with a very heavy stone, he told Benny to drop smaller stones into the water.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch8_3.mp3" %}

"Splash them right in, Benny! Keep them in a straight line between these two trees." Jess took off Benny’s shirt and pants to wash them in the stream. "Good time to wash them!" she said. Violet offered to do the washing so Jess could help build the dam. The children built a wall of stones under the water to give the logs a solid base. Finally, it was time to place the logs.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch8_4.mp3" %}

Jess had a great idea: "Let’s wedge the first log between these two trees. The harder the water pushes against it, the tighter it will stay." "That is a great plan!" Henry said. When they placed the first log, water began to leak out of the holes. Henry decided they needed more logs. They stacked three logs, then another three, and then three more on top.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch8_5.mp3" %}

The children worked hard, plugging every hole with stones, branches, and mud. Benny helped by dropping stones into the leaks. Finally, they sat back to watch the pool fill. The water rose until it spilled over the top of the logs like a beautiful waterfall. Henry measured the depth again. It was almost three feet deep—perfect! The girls had to go prepare dinner, but Henry stayed to swim.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch8_6.mp3" %}

They had a great feast that night, including the rest of the stew, bread, butter, and two doughnuts cut into rings for dessert. Violet rang their new "dinner bell"—a piece of steel hung on a tree branch—to call the boys. That day, the girls decided to cut their hair. It was long and tangling, making it difficult to work in the woods. Jess used Violet’s small scissors to cut their hair evenly. They checked their reflection in the brook and were very happy with their new, practical look.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch8_7.mp3" %}

In the afternoon, they went for a long walk. They found two amazing things. First, they found a hollow tree full of walnuts. Second, they found a runaway hen! Watch the dog barked, and the hen ran away, leaving behind a nest with five eggs. "Eggs!" Benny cheered. "It’s a gift from heaven," Violet said. They scrambled the eggs in the big kettle with butter, milk, and salt. It was a delicious meal. "We will have to eat just bread and milk tomorrow," Jess said. But as you will see, they ended up having much more than that.
