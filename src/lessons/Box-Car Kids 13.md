One day, the doctor asked Henry to clean his small laboratory. Henry spent the whole morning washing bottles, labeling them, and cleaning tools. He carefully saved any broken glass or supplies he found and brought them home to his family. While Henry was working, he heard a young man in the office talking to the doctor. "Can you tell me if this is real ginseng?" the man asked.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch13_1.mp3" %}

"Yes, it is," the doctor said. "You can get two dollars a pound for the root at any drugstore." Henry peeked and saw the plant. It had a white, feathery flower. Henry realized it was the same plant Violet had in her vase at home! When the young man left, Henry said, "I know where I can find a lot of that plant." "Is that right?" the doctor said kindly.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch13_2.mp3" %}

"Only the root is valuable, but anyone who wants to dig it up can sell as much as they want." When Henry went home for lunch, he told his sisters. They immediately went to work with knives, iron spoons, and their kettle. With Benny running around finding every white flower he could see, they dug up a large amount of ginseng root. Henry was so happy when he saw their hard work.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch13_3.mp3" %}

The next day, he took the roots to a drugstore and sold them for three dollars. Henry immediately went to the store and bought a pair of new brown socks for Benny. Benny was so happy that he wouldn't stop talking about his wonderful new socks. Benny had been sad about something else for a while. When the children had run away from the bakery, Jess forgot to bring Benny’s favorite Teddy bear.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch13_4.mp3" %}

It was a very old, worn-out bear with no eyes and a limp body, but Benny had loved it for three years. He had been very sad sleeping in a strange place without it. Jess had a plan. She washed Benny’s old, worn-out brown socks and sat down with Violet. They were going to make him a new bear! With Benny watching, Jess used the socks to make the shape of a bear.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch13_5.mp3" %}

One sock became the head and body, and the other was used for the arms, legs, and stuffing. Jess worked hard to make the head, while Violet stitched beautiful eyes and a nose with black thread. "You must make a tail, too, Jessy," Benny said. "Bears don't usually have tails, Benny," Jess said. But Benny insisted, so she made a long, thin tail and sewed it on tightly.

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch13_6.mp3" %}

She added floppy ears and a red string collar. "What is his name, Jessy?" Benny asked when she handed him the finished bear. "He is a new bear, so we shouldn't call him Teddy," Jess said. "No, this is not Teddy," Benny agreed. "This one has a pretty tail." "We sold the ginseng to buy your new socks," Jess said. "And because we have your new socks, we were able to use the old ones to make this bear."

{% audioPlayer "/audio/bck-ch13_7.mp3" %}

"You want his name to be Stockings?" Benny asked politely. "No," Jess said. "I was thinking of 'Ginseng'." "Ginseng?" Benny thought about it. "That is a nice name. All right, Ginseng will be a good bear, as long as Watch the dog doesn't bark at him." From that day on, the bear's name was Ginseng, and he lived to be a very old bear indeed.
