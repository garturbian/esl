Once upon a time, in a small village near the jungle, there lived some naughty monkeys. These monkeys loved to eat and would often try to get food from the villagers.

1: Where did the monkeys live?  
2: What was near the village?  
3: What were the monkeys like?  
4: What did the monkeys love to eat?  
5: Who did the monkeys try to get food from?  
6: Did the monkeys ask politely for food?  
7: Were the monkeys good or naughty?  
8: Why did the monkeys visit the villagers?  
9: Was the village big or small?  
10: What do you think the villagers felt about the monkeys?


{% audioPlayer "/audio/naughty-monkeys_1.mp3" %}

One sunny morning, a little boy named Tim went out to play. He brought his lunch with him: an apple and some cookies. As he sat under a big tree, enjoying his snack, a group of hungry monkeys noticed his food.

1: What time of day did Tim go out to play?  
2: What was the weather like that morning?  
3: What is the boy’s name in the story?  
4: What did Tim bring with him for lunch?  
5: Where did Tim sit to eat his snack?  
6: What was Tim doing under the big tree?  
7: Who noticed Tim’s food?  
8: What kind of animals saw the lunch?  
9: Why do you think the monkeys came closer?  
10: What foods did Tim have in his lunch?


{% audioPlayer "/audio/naughty-monkeys_2.mp3" %}

First, a small monkey approached timidly. It looked at the delicious fruit and then at Tim. The monkey was afraid that Tim would be mad if it took his food directly. So, the little monkey decided to wait until Tim wasn't looking.

1: Which animal came closer first?  
2: How did the small monkey move toward Tim?  
3: What did the monkey look at on the ground?  
4: Who did the monkey look at after the fruit?  
5: Was the monkey brave or afraid?  
6: What did the monkey think Tim might feel?  
7: What did the monkey want to take from Tim?  
8: Why did the monkey not take the food directly?  
9: What did the monkey decide to do instead?  
10: When did the monkey plan to take the food?


{% audioPlayer "/audio/naughty-monkeys_3.mp3" %}

After a few minutes, another monkey came along with some friends. Seeing an opportunity, they started to climb up the tree where Tim's lunch was. They peeked down at him and saw he had turned away for a moment. The monkeys got excited and jumped down quickly!

1: How many monkeys came after a few minutes?
2: Did the new monkeys come alone or with friends?
3: What did the monkeys start to climb?
4: What were the monkeys looking for in the tree?
5: What did the monkeys see Tim doing?
6: Why was Tim not looking at his lunch?
7: How did the monkeys feel when they saw the chance?
8: What did the monkeys do after jumping down?
9: Where was Tim’s lunch placed?
10: Do you think the monkeys were being careful or rushed?

{% audioPlayer "/audio/naughty-monkeys_4.mp3" %}

They began to eat the food without thinking about it. Tim came back, looking for his snack. He found some crumbs but no whole cookies or apple. He looked up into the tree, where he could see the grinning faces of the naughty monkeys.

1: What did the monkeys start doing with the food?  
2: Did the monkeys think before eating the food?  
3: What did Tim come back to look for?  
4: What did Tim find instead of his snack?  
5: Where did Tim look after finding only crumbs?  
6: What did Tim see in the tree?  
7: How were the monkeys feeling when Tim saw them?  
8: What was missing from Tim’s lunch?  
9: Why do you think Tim looked up into the tree?  
10: How do you think Tim felt when he saw the monkeys?


{% audioPlayer "/audio/naughty-monkeys_5.mp3" %}

Tim tried to shout at them, but they just laughed and jumped away. They had enjoyed their stolen treat! In the end, Tim was a bit upset that his food was taken, but the villagers knew how much the monkeys loved fruit and snacks. So, every now and then, someone would give some extra food for these hungry monkeys.

1: What did Tim try to do when he saw the monkeys?  
2: How did the monkeys react to Tim shouting?  
3: What did the monkeys do after laughing?  
4: What did the monkeys enjoy doing with the food?  
5: How did Tim feel at the end of the story?  
6: Why was Tim upset?  
7: What did the villagers understand about the monkeys?  
8: What kind of food did the monkeys like?  
9: What did people sometimes give to the monkeys?  
10: What do you think about the villagers’ solution?


{% audioPlayer "/audio/naughty-monkeys_6.mp3" %}

The village children learned to be careful with their food around the monkeys and not leave it where they could see. But the naughty monkeys continued to play a fun trick on people every once in a while, making everyone smile. And that's how the little boy Tim and his villagers had to deal with some cheeky monkeys in their neighborhood.

1: What did the village children learn about their food?
2: Where should they not leave their food?
3: Why did they need to be careful with their food?
4: What did the naughty monkeys continue to do?
5: How often did the monkeys play tricks on people?
6: Did the tricks make people angry or smile?
7: How did the villagers feel about the monkeys in the end?
8: Who is Tim in the story?
9: What problem did the village have with monkeys?
10: What is the main idea of the story?
