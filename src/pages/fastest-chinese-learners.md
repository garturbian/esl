嗨，好久不見朋友們，今年是我教漢語的第五年了，  
hāi , hǎo jiǔ bú jiàn péng yǒu men , jīn nián shì wǒ jiāo hàn yǔ de dì wǔ nián le ,  
Hi, long time no see friends. This year is my fifth year teaching Chinese.  

目前有幾千個小時的教學經驗，  
mù qián yǒu jǐ qiān gè xiǎo shí de jiào xué jīng yàn ,  
Right now, I have several thousand hours of teaching experience.  

我在線上一對一教成人，  
wǒ zài xiàn shàng yí duì yī jiāo chéng rén ,  
I teach adults one-on-one online.  

也在線下的國際學校教小孩子學漢語，  
yě zài xiàn xià de guó jì xué xiào jiāo xiǎo hái zi xué hàn yǔ ,  
I also teach children Chinese at an international school offline.  

見過使用各種各樣的方式學習漢語的學生，  
jiàn guò shǐ yòng gè zhǒng gè yàng de fāng shì xué xí hàn yǔ de xué shēng ,  
I have met students who learn Chinese in many different ways.  

但我發現其中把漢語學得又輕鬆又好的學生都有一些共同點。  
dàn wǒ fā xiàn qí zhōng bǎ hàn yǔ xué de yòu qīng sōng yòu hǎo de xué shēng dōu yǒu yì xiē gòng tóng diǎn 。  
But I found that the students who learn Chinese easily and well all have some things in common.  

今天我想跟大家聊聊這些共同點。  
jīn tiān wǒ xiǎng gēn dà jiā liáo liáo zhè xiē gòng tóng diǎn 。  
Today I want to talk with everyone about these common points.  

第一個共同點是，他們都喜歡聽漢語。  
dì yī gè gòng tóng diǎn shì ， tā men dōu xǐ huān tīng hàn yǔ 。  
The first common point is that they all like listening to Chinese.  

漢語是一門有聲調的語言，  
hàn yǔ shì yì mén yǒu shēng diào de yǔ yán ,  
Chinese is a tonal language.  

相同的拼音不同的聲調是完全不同的意思，  
xiāng tóng de pīn yīn bù tóng de shēng diào shì wán quán bù tóng de yì si ,  
The same pinyin with different tones has completely different meanings.  

所以在學習第一個詞語時，  
suǒ yǐ zài xué xí dì yí gè cí yǔ shí ,  
So when learning the first word,  

就必須要發出正確的聲調，  
jiù bì xū yào fā chū zhèng què de shēng diào ,  
you must pronounce the correct tones.  

而發出正確的聲調的前提是能夠辨別聲調。  
ér fā chū zhèng què de shēng diào de qián tí shì néng gòu biàn bié shēng diào 。  
And the key to producing correct tones is being able to recognize tones.  

對於一些母語裡沒有聲調的學習者來說，  
duì yú yì xiē mǔ yǔ lǐ méi yǒu shēng diào de xué xí zhě lái shuō ,  
For some learners whose native language does not have tones,  

這是非常困難的，  
zhè shì fēi cháng kùn nán de ,  
this is very difficult.  

所以必須聽了足夠多的漢語才能讓這一步更順利。  
suǒ yǐ bì xū tīng le zú gòu duō de hàn yǔ cái néng ràng zhè yí bù gèng shùn lì 。  
So they must listen to enough Chinese to make this step easier.  

在初期能夠把四個聲調發清楚的學生，  
zài chū qī néng gòu bǎ sì gè shēng diào fā qīng chǔ de xué shēng ,  
Students who can pronounce the four tones clearly in the beginning  

zài zhī hòu de liàn xí zhōng zhú jiàn gǒng gù ,  
gradually strengthen them during later practice.  

發音越來越標準。  
fā yīn yuè lái yuè biāo zhǔn 。  
Their pronunciation becomes more and more standard.  

反之，  
fǎn zhī ,  
On the other hand,  

前期沒有把四個聲調發清楚的學生，  
qián qī méi yǒu bǎ sì gè shēng diào fā qīng chǔ de xué shēng ,  
students who did not pronounce the four tones clearly in the beginning  

zài liàn xí de guò chéng zhōng bú duàn gǒng gù ,  
keep reinforcing them during practice.  

這也會鞏固錯誤的聲調，  
zhè yě huì gǒng gù cuò wù de shēng diào ,  
This also reinforces wrong tones.  

導致後期要花非常非常多的時間去糾正。  
dǎo zhì hòu qī yào huā fēi cháng fēi cháng duō de shí jiān qù jiū zhèng 。  
As a result, later they must spend a huge amount of time correcting them.  

在之後的學習過程中，  
zài zhī hòu de xué xí guò chéng zhōng ,  
Later in the learning process,  

我發現那些喜歡看中文影片或聽中文播客的學生，  
wǒ fā xiàn nà xiē xǐ huān kàn zhōng wén yǐng piàn huò tīng zhōng wén bō kè de xué shēng ,  
I found that students who like watching Chinese videos or listening to Chinese podcasts  

不僅發音明顯比聽得比較少的學生標準很多，  
bù jǐn fā yīn míng xiǎn bǐ tīng de bǐ jiào shǎo de xué shēng biāo zhǔn hěn duō ,  
not only have much better pronunciation than students who listen less,  

學習詞語和語法時也更快。  
xué xí cí yǔ hé yǔ fǎ shí yě gèng kuài 。  
they also learn vocabulary and grammar faster.  

我的英語也是在我大量聽英語素材後，  
wǒ de yīng yǔ yě shì zài wǒ dà liàng tīng yīng yǔ sù cái hòu ,  
My English also improved after I listened to a lot of English material.  

聽力從A2的水平提升。  
tīng lì cóng A2 de shuǐ píng tí shēng 。  
My listening improved from an A2 level.  

我曾經每天除了工作時間都放著英語影片，  
wǒ céng jīng měi tiān chú le gōng zuò shí jiān dōu fàng zhe yīng yǔ yǐng piàn ,  
I once played English videos every day except during work.  

剛開始 I 只能理解10%，  
gāng kāi shǐ wǒ zhǐ néng lǐ jiě shí fēn zhī shí ,  
At first I could only understand 10%.  

後來我能理解50%，  
hòu lái wǒ néng lǐ jiě bǎi fēn zhī wǔ shí ,  
Later I could understand 50%.  

再後來我能理解80%，  
zài hòu lái wǒ néng lǐ jiě bǎi fēn zhī bā shí ,  
After that I could understand 80%.  

現在出現在我 YouTube 首頁的影片，  
xiàn zài chū xiàn zài wǒ YouTube shǒu yè de yǐng piàn ,  
Now the videos that appear on my YouTube homepage  

我幾乎都能理解90%到100%。  
wǒ jī hū dōu néng lǐ jiě bǎi fēn zhī jiǔ shí dào yì bǎi fēn zhī bǎi 。  
I can understand almost 90% to 100% of them.  

因為我早上化妝的動作比較慢，  
yīn wèi wǒ zǎo shàng huà zhuāng de dòng zuò bǐ jiào màn ,  
Because I get ready slowly in the morning,  

所以我會把播客1.5倍速播放，  
suǒ yǐ wǒ huì bǎ bō kè yì diǎn wǔ bèi sù bō fàng ,  
so I play podcasts at 1.5x speed.  

幫助我加快我的動作，  
bāng zhù wǒ jiā kuài wǒ de dòng zuò ,  
This helps me move faster.  

也繼續練習我的聽力。  
yě jì xù liàn xí wǒ de tīng lì 。  
It also helps me continue practicing my listening.  

即使我不總是專注地聽播客和影片裡在說什麼，  
jí shǐ wǒ bù zǒng shì zhuān zhù de tīng bō kè hé yǐng piàn lǐ zài shuō shén me ,  
Even if I am not always fully focused on what the podcasts and videos are saying,  

但當我跟朋友或者老師聊天的時候，  
dàn dāng wǒ gēn péng yǒu huò zhě lǎo shī liáo tiān de shí hòu ,  
when I talk with friends or teachers,  

我發現我竟然自然地使用一些我沒學過的更高級的結構。  
wǒ fā xiàn wǒ jìng rán zì rán de shǐ yòng yì xiē wǒ méi xué guò de gèng gāo jí de jié gòu 。  
I found that I naturally used some more advanced structures that I had never studied.

第二個共同點是，他們都會練習說。  
dì èr gè gòng tóng diǎn shì ， tā men dōu huì liàn xí shuō 。  
The second common point is that they all practice speaking.  

有的學生喜歡跟朋友或者老師聊天，  
yǒu de xué shēng xǐ huān gēn péng yǒu huò zhě lǎo shī liáo tiān ,  
Some students like chatting with friends or teachers.  

有的學生會跟AI或者自己聊天。  
yǒu de xué shēng huì gēn AI huò zhě zì jǐ liáo tiān 。  
Some students chat with AI or with themselves.  

無論是哪一種方式都能練習使用新的語言，  
wú lùn shì nǎ yì zhǒng fāng shì dōu néng liàn xí shǐ yòng xīn de yǔ yán ,  
No matter which method they use, they can practice using a new language.  

把心裡所想的有邏輯地表達出來。  
bǎ xīn lǐ suǒ xiǎng de yǒu luó jí de biǎo dá chū lái 。  
They can express what they think in a logical way.  

即使是母語，  
jí shǐ shì mǔ yǔ ,  
Even in a native language,  

表達能力也是需要練習的，  
biǎo dá néng lì yě shì xū yào liàn xí de ,  
communication skills still need practice.  

更別說是一門新的語言。  
gèng bié shuō shì yì mén xīn de yǔ yán 。  
This is even more true for a new language.  

這一點我再贊同不過了。  
zhè yì diǎn wǒ zài zàn tóng bú guò le 。  
I completely agree with this point.  

我記得有一段時間我不喜歡跟人聊天，  
wǒ jì de yǒu yí duàn shí jiān wǒ bù xǐ huān gēn rén liáo tiān ,  
I remember there was a period when I did not like talking with people.  

wǒ zhǐ néng shuō wǒ xǐ huān liáo tiān 。  
I could only say that I liked chatting.  

我記得有一段時間我不喜歡跟人聊天，  
wǒ jì de yǒu yí duàn shí jiān wǒ bù xǐ huān gēn rén liáo tiān ,  
I remember there was a period when I did not like talking with people.  

wǒ zhǐ néng shuō wǒ xǐ huān liáo tiān 。  
I could only say that I liked chatting.  

我記得有一段時間我不喜歡跟人聊天，  
wǒ jì de yǒu yí duàn shí jiān wǒ bù xǐ huān gēn rén liáo tiān ,  
I remember there was a period when I did not like talking with people.  

每天說的話很少。  
měi tiān shuō de huà hěn shǎo 。  
I spoke very little every day.  

在開始跟人交流的時候，  
zài kāi shǐ gēn rén jiāo liú de shí hòu ,  
When I started communicating with people,  

我發現我竟然不能流暢地表達自己的想法。  
wǒ fā xiàn wǒ jìng rán bù néng liú chàng de biǎo dá zì jǐ de xiǎng fǎ 。  
I found that I could not express my thoughts fluently.  

所以想要用好一門語言，  
suǒ yǐ xiǎng yào yòng hǎo yì mén yǔ yán ,  
So if you want to use a language well,  

離不開平時的練習。  
lí bù kāi píng shí de liàn xí 。  
you cannot avoid regular practice.  

第三個共同點是，  
dì sān gè gòng tóng diǎn shì ，  
The third common point is  

他們都學一點點語法。  
tā men dōu xué yì diǎn diǎn yǔ fǎ 。  
they all study a little grammar.  

我見過一些華裔，  
wǒ jiàn guò yì xiē huá yì ,  
I have met some overseas Chinese people.  

他們平時總是聽父母說漢語。  
tā men píng shí zǒng shì tīng fù mǔ shuō hàn yǔ 。  
They often hear their parents speaking Chinese.  

說漢語時還是會有一些基礎的語法。  
shuō hàn yǔ shí hái shì huì yǒu yì xiē jī chǔ de yǔ fǎ 。  
When they speak Chinese, they still use some basic grammar.  

也有一些學生讀了很多書，  
yě yǒu yì xiē xué shēng dú le hěn duō shū ,  
There are also some students who read many books  

看了很多影片。  
kàn le hěn duō yǐng piàn 。  
and watched many videos.  

說話時，  
shuō huà shí ,  
When they speak,  

詞語都對，  
cí yǔ dōu duì ,  
their vocabulary is correct,  

但一聽他們說話，  
dàn yì tīng tā men shuō huà ,  
but once you hear them speak,  

一聽他們說漢語，  
yì tīng tā men shuō hàn yǔ ,  
once you hear them speak Chinese,  

就知道他們的母語是英語。  
jiù zhī dào tā men de mǔ yǔ shì yīng yǔ 。  
you know their native language is English.  

其實他們需要學習的只是在一開始，  
qí shí tā men xū yào xué xí de zhǐ shì zài yì kāi shǐ ,  
Actually, what they need to learn is only at the beginning,  

學習一點點語法。  
xué xí yì diǎn diǎn yǔ fǎ 。  
to study a little grammar.  

這跟聲調一樣，  
zhè gēn shēng diào yí yàng ,  
This is the same as tones.  

一開始的基礎決定了之後不斷重複的是什麼結構。  
yì kāi shǐ de jī chǔ jué dìng le zhī hòu bú duàn chóng fù de shì shén me jié gòu 。  
The foundation at the beginning decides what structures you repeat later.  

這跟聲調一樣，  
zhè gēn shēng diào yí yàng ,  
This is the same as tones.  

一開始的基礎決定了之後不斷重複的是什麼結構。  
yì kāi shǐ de jī chǔ jué dìng le zhī hòu bú duàn chóng fù de shì shén me jié gòu 。  
The foundation at the beginning decides what structures you repeat later.  

wǒ zhī suǒ yǐ shuō shì yì diǎn diǎn ,  
The reason I say “a little”  

是因為漢語的語法真的太少了。  
shì yīn wèi hàn yǔ de yǔ fǎ zhēn de tài shǎo le 。  
is because Chinese grammar is really very simple.  

有的人甚至說漢語沒有語法，  
yǒu de rén shèn zhì shuō hàn yǔ méi yǒu yǔ fǎ ,  
Some people even say Chinese has no grammar.  

因為漢語幾乎沒有單複數，  
yīn wèi hàn yǔ jī hū méi yǒu dān fù shù ,  
Because Chinese almost has no singular or plural forms,  

沒有時態，  
méi yǒu shí tài ,  
no verb tense,  

沒有主位變形，  
méi yǒu zhǔ wèi biàn xíng ,  
no subject changes,  

沒有動詞變形。  
méi yǒu dòng cí biàn xíng 。  
and no verb conjugation.  

無論是疑問句還是陳述句，  
wú lùn shì yí wèn jù hái shì chén shù jù ,  
Whether it is a question sentence or a statement sentence,  

都是一樣的句子結構。  
dōu shì yí yàng de jù zi jié gòu 。  
the sentence structure is the same.  

所以其實這些基礎的，  
suǒ yǐ qí shí zhè xiē jī chǔ de ,  
So actually these basic grammar points,  

後期最難糾正的語法，  
hòu qī zuì nán jiū zhèng de yǔ fǎ ,  
which are hardest to fix later,  

只需要在一個小時內就能講完。  
zhǐ xū yào zài yí gè xiǎo shí nèi jiù néng jiǎng wán 。  
can be explained within one hour.  

第四個共同點是，  
dì sì gè gòng tóng diǎn shì ，  
The fourth common point is  

認識漢字很快的學生都會寫一點點漢字。  
rèn shi hàn zì hěn kuài de xué shēng dōu huì xiě yì diǎn diǎn hàn zì 。  
Students who learn Chinese characters quickly all write a little Chinese.  

很多人覺得漢字太難了，  
hěn duō rén jué de hàn zì tài nán le ,  
Many people think Chinese characters are too difficult.  

那麼多漢字花那麼多時間學習，  
nà me duō hàn zì huā nà me duō shí jiān xué xí ,  
There are so many characters and they take so much time to learn.  

甚至寫出來，  
shèn zhì xiě chū lái ,  
Even writing them  

簡直太不划算了。  
jiǎn zhí tài bù huá suàn le 。  
feels completely not worth it.  

其實他們不知道，  
qí shí tā men bù zhī dào ,  
Actually, they do not know  

當你每天開始寫幾個詞語，  
dāng nǐ měi tiān kāi shǐ xiě jǐ gè cí yǔ ,  
that when you start writing a few words every day,  

一段時間後，

after some time,

even if you did not study the rules of Chinese characters,  

你會發現很多詞語裡有共同的漢字。

you will notice that many words share common characters.  

這些漢字可以幫助你學習漢字。  
zhè xiē hàn zì kě yǐ bāng zhù nǐ xué xí hàn zì 。  
These characters can help you learn Chinese characters.  

這些漢字可以幫助你學習漢字的規則。  
zhè xiē hàn zì kě yǐ bāng zhù nǐ xué xí hàn zì de guī zé 。  
These characters can help you learn the rules of Chinese characters.  

這些漢字可以幫助你猜到這個詞語的意思。  
zhè xiē hàn zì kě yǐ bāng zhù nǐ cāi dào zhè gè cí yǔ de yì si 。  
These characters can help you guess the meaning of a word.  

你會發現一個漢字可以拆分成不同的部件，  
nǐ huì fā xiàn yí gè hàn zì kě yǐ chāi fēn chéng bù tóng de bù jiàn ,  
You will discover that one character can be divided into different parts.  

一些部件反覆出現，

Some parts appear again and again.  

它們代表特定的意思。  
tā men dài biǎo tè dìng de yì si 。  
They represent specific meanings.  

你可以通過它們猜到漢字的意思或者讀音。  
nǐ kě yǐ tōng guò tā men cāi dào hàn zì de yì si huò zhě dú yīn 。  
You can use them to guess the meaning or pronunciation of a character.  

你會發現所有的漢字其實都是由兩個基本的筆畫組成的。  
nǐ huì fā xiàn suǒ yǒu de hàn zì qí shí dōu shì yóu liǎng gè jī běn de bǐ huà zǔ chéng de 。  
You will discover that all Chinese characters are made from two basic strokes.  

你學習的漢字越多，

The more Chinese characters you learn,  

學習漢語越快。

the faster you learn Chinese.
