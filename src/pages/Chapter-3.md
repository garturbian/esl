<div class="lesson-text">
星工作  

xīng gōngzuò
Star Job

0:00 謝先生坐在那裡
Xiè xiānsheng zuò zài nàlǐ
Mr. Xie sat there.

0:03 看著手裡的報紙
kànzhe shǒu lǐ de bàozhǐ
Looking at the newspaper in his hands.

0:05 他想了一會兒
tā xiǎng le yíhuìr
He thought for a moment.

0:06 然後說
ránhòu shuō
Then he said,

0:07 我以後不想去面試這樣的工作了
wǒ yǐhòu bù xiǎng qù miànshì zhèyàng de gōngzuò le
I do not want to interview for this kind of job again in the future.

0:10 那麼多卷髮的男人都去那裡面試
nàme duō juǎnfǎ de nánrén dōu qù nàlǐ miànshì
So many curly-haired men went there for interviews.

0:13 如果不是因為這個面試
rúguǒ búshì yīnwèi zhège miànshì
If it were not because of this interview,

0:15 我都不知道上海有這麼多卷髮男人
wǒ dōu bù zhīdào Shànghǎi yǒu zhème duō juǎnfǎ nánrén
I would not have known that Shanghai has so many curly-haired men.

0:18 他們的頭髮有的不太卷
tāmen de tóufa yǒude bú tài juǎn
Some of their hair was not very curly.

0:21 有的很卷
yǒude hěn juǎn
Some was very curly.

0:22 但是像劉璐飛說的那樣
dànshì xiàng Liú Lùfēi shuō de nàyàng
But just like Liu Lufei said,

0:24 他們很多都是身體不太好的老人
tāmen hěnduō dōu shì shēntǐ bú tài hǎo de lǎorén
Many of them were old people in poor health.

0:27 他們都想要這個工作
tāmen dōu xiǎng yào zhège gōngzuò
They all wanted this job.

0:29 那麼多卷髮的人來面試
nàme duō juǎnfǎ de rén lái miànshì
So many curly-haired people came for the interview.

0:31 我覺得他們不一定會要我
wǒ juéde tāmen bù yídìng huì yào wǒ
I felt they might not choose me.

0:34 我也不喜歡去人多的地方
wǒ yě bù xǐhuān qù rén duō de dìfang
I also do not like going to crowded places.

0:36 想到這些
xiǎngdào zhèxiē
Thinking about these things,

0:37 我有點想回家了
wǒ yǒudiǎn xiǎng huí jiā le
I kind of wanted to go home.

0:39 那後來呢
nà hòulái ne
What happened after that?

0:40 高明馬上問
Gāo Míng mǎshàng wèn
Gao Ming immediately asked.

0:42 但是劉璐飛不同意
dànshì Liú Lùfēi bù tóngyì
But Liu Lufei did not agree.

0:44 他一定讓我去試一試
tā yídìng ràng wǒ qù shì yíshì
He insisted that I give it a try.

0:47 謝先生又說
Xiè xiānsheng yòu shuō
Mr. Xie said again,

0:48 他帶我慢慢地往辦公室走
tā dài wǒ mànmàn de wǎng bàngōngshì zǒu
He slowly led me toward the office.

0:51 快到辦公室的時候
kuài dào bàngōngshì de shíhou
When we were almost at the office,

0:52 我們看到一些人很開心地往辦公室裡走
wǒmen kàndào yìxiē rén hěn kāixīn de wǎng bàngōngshì lǐ zǒu
We saw some people happily walking into the office.

0:56 還有一些人很難過地
háiyǒu yìxiē rén hěn nánguò de
And some people sadly

0:58 從辦公室裡出來
cóng bàngōngshì lǐ chūlái
came out of the office.

1:00 很快我們也在辦公室裡了
hěn kuài wǒmen yě zài bàngōngshì lǐ le
Soon we were also inside the office.

1:03 怎麼樣
zěnmeyàng
How was it?

1:04 你你是他們想找的人嗎
nǐ shì tāmen xiǎng zhǎo de rén ma
Were you the person they were looking for?

1:06 趙亮很想知道後面的事
Zhào Liàng hěn xiǎng zhīdào hòumiàn de shì
Zhao Liang really wanted to know what happened next.

1:08 你聽我慢慢說
nǐ tīng wǒ mànmàn shuō
Listen to me slowly explain it.

1:10 謝先生不太喜歡趙亮
Xiè xiānsheng bú tài xǐhuān Zhào Liàng
Mr. Xie did not like Zhao Liang very much.

1:12 他覺得趙亮總是問很多問題
tā juéde Zhào Liàng zǒngshì wèn hěn duō wèntí
He felt Zhao Liang always asked many questions.

1:15 他喝了一點茶
tā hē le yìdiǎn chá
He drank a little tea.

1:16 然後說
ránhòu shuō
Then he said,

1:17 他們的辦公室很奇怪
tāmen de bàngōngshì hěn qíguài
Their office was very strange.

1:19 只有一個大桌子和幾個椅子
zhǐyǒu yíge dà zhuōzi hé jǐge yǐzi
There was only one big table and a few chairs.

1:22 沒有別的東西
méiyǒu biéde dōngxi
There was nothing else.

1:23 他們的老闆也是一個卷髮男人
tāmen de lǎobǎn yě shì yíge juǎnfǎ nánrén
Their boss was also a curly-haired man.

1:26 他坐在椅子上
tā zuò zài yǐzi shàng
He sat on a chair.

1:27 每一個卷髮男人
měi yíge juǎnfǎ nánrén
Every curly-haired man

1:28 都要走到桌子前跟老闆說話
dōu yào zǒu dào zhuōzi qián gēn lǎobǎn shuōhuà
had to walk to the table and talk with the boss.

1:32 但是那個老闆總是能找到那些卷髮男人的問題
dànshì nàge lǎobǎn zǒngshì néng zhǎodào nàxiē juǎnfǎ nánrén de wèntí
But that boss could always find problems with those curly-haired men.

1:37 然後讓他們出去
ránhòu ràng tāmen chūqù
Then he made them leave.

1:38 但是
dànshì
But—

1:39 但是什麼
dànshì shénme
But what?

1:41 趙亮又問
Zhào Liàng yòu wèn
Zhao Liang asked again.

1:42 我走過去的時候
wǒ zǒu guòqù de shíhou
When I walked over,

1:44 劉璐飛對他說
Liú Lùfēi duì tā shuō
Liu Lufei said to him,

1:45 這是我的老闆謝先生
zhè shì wǒ de lǎobǎn Xiè xiānsheng
This is my boss, Mr. Xie.

1:47 他希望在這裡做助理
tā xīwàng zài zhèlǐ zuò zhùlǐ
He hopes to work here as an assistant.

1:49 劉璐飛剛說完
Liú Lùfēi gāng shuō wán
As soon as Liu Lufei finished speaking,

1:50 那個老闆一下子很開心
nàge lǎobǎn yíxiàzi hěn kāixīn
That boss suddenly became very happy.

1:53 他馬上對我說太好了
tā mǎshàng duì wǒ shuō tài hǎo le
He immediately said to me, “Wonderful!”

1:55 你就是我們要找的助理
nǐ jiùshì wǒmen yào zhǎo de zhùlǐ
You are exactly the assistant we are looking for.

1:58 你的頭髮
nǐ de tóufa
Your hair

1:58 又黑又卷
yòu hēi yòu juǎn
is black and curly.

1:59 很漂亮
hěn piàoliang
Very beautiful.

2:00 然後他走到門外
ránhòu tā zǒu dào ménwài
Then he walked outside the door.

2:02 對別的來面試的人說
duì biéde lái miànshì de rén shuō
He said to the other people who came for interviews,

2:04 你們可以回去了
nǐmen kěyǐ huíqù le
You can go home now.

2:05 我已經找到最好的助理了
wǒ yǐjīng zhǎodào zuì hǎo de zhùlǐ le
I have already found the best assistant.

2:07 高明笑了一下
Gāo Míng xiào le yíxià
Gao Ming smiled a little.

2:09 對謝先生說
duì Xiè xiānsheng shuō
He said to Mr. Xie,

2:10 對你來說
duì nǐ lái shuō
For you,

2:12 找到這個工作一點也不難
zhǎodào zhège gōngzuò yìdiǎn yě bù nán
getting this job was not difficult at all.

2:14 對
duì
Right.

2:15 謝先生說
Xiè xiānsheng shuō
Mr. Xie said,

2:16 他們讓我第二天就去上班
tāmen ràng wǒ dì èr tiān jiù qù shàngbān
They asked me to start work the next day.

2:18 每天上午八點到下午兩點
měitiān shàngwǔ bā diǎn dào xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn
Every day from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m.

2:21 工作是看書寫東西
gōngzuò shì kàn shū xiě dōngxi
The work was reading books and writing things.

2:23 可是
kěshì
But,

2:24 我想到我還要開茶館
wǒ xiǎngdào wǒ hái yào kāi cháguǎn
I thought about how I still needed to run the tea house.

2:26 可能沒辦法在這個時間上工作了
kěnéng méi bànfǎ zài zhège shíjiān shàng gōngzuò le
Maybe I could not work during those hours.

2:28 這個時候劉璐飛馬上說
zhège shíhou Liú Lùfēi mǎshàng shuō
At that moment Liu Lufei immediately said,

2:31 放心吧老闆
fàngxīn ba lǎobǎn
Do not worry, boss.

2:33 平常下午三點以後才有人來茶館
píngcháng xiàwǔ sān diǎn yǐhòu cái yǒu rén lái cháguǎn
Usually people only come to the tea house after 3 p.m.

2:37 再說我一直都在茶館裡
zàishuō wǒ yìzhí dōu zài cháguǎn lǐ
Besides, I am always at the tea house.

2:39 聽他這麼說
tīng tā zhème shuō
Hearing him say this,

2:41 我馬上同意第二天就去上班
wǒ mǎshàng tóngyì dì èr tiān jiù qù shàngbān
I immediately agreed to start work the next day.

2:43 這麼奇怪的面試
zhème qíguài de miànshì
Such a strange interview.

2:45 我真想聽你說說
wǒ zhēn xiǎng tīng nǐ shuōshuo
I really want to hear you talk about it.

2:47 你在新興卷髮公司做什麼
nǐ zài Xīnxīng Juǎnfǎ Gōngsī zuò shénme
What did you do at the Xinxing Curling Company?

2:49 高明說
Gāo Míng shuō
Gao Ming said.

2:50 他覺得這個案子越來越有意思了
tā juéde zhège ànzi yuèláiyuè yǒuyìsi le
He felt this case was becoming more and more interesting.

2:53 我從來沒有聽說過這麼奇怪的公司
wǒ cónglái méiyǒu tīngshuō guò zhème qíguài de gōngcī
I have never heard of such a strange company.

2:56 也從來沒有做過這麼奇怪的工作
yě cónglái méiyǒu zuò guò zhème qíguài de gōngzuò
And I have never done such a strange job.

2:58 我相信你們的想法也跟我一樣
wǒ xiāngxìn nǐmen de xiǎngfǎ yě gēn wǒ yíyàng
I believe your thoughts are the same as mine.

3:01 謝先生坐在椅子上
Xiè xiānsheng zuò zài yǐzi shàng
Mr. Xie sat on the chair.

3:03 慢慢地說
mànmàn de shuō
And slowly said.
