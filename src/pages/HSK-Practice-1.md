<div class="lesson-text">
0:00 大家好，我是李揚。
dàjiā hǎo, wǒ shì Lǐ Yáng.
Hello everyone, I am Li Yang.

0:03 大家好，我是安娜。
dàjiā hǎo, wǒ shì Ānnà.
Hello everyone, I am Anna.

0:05 今天我們來做一些HSK三級的練習。
jīntiān wǒmen lái zuò yīxiē HSK sān jí de liànxí.
Today we are going to do some HSK level 3 exercises.

0:09 對，聽力練習。
duì, tīnglì liànxí.
Yes, listening exercises.

0:11 我會說一個句子，然後安娜會問一個問題。
wǒ huì shuō yī gè jùzi, ránhòu Ānnà huì wèn yī gè wèntí.
I will say a sentence, and then Anna will ask a question.

0:16 大家聽完以後，請選出正確的答案。
dàjiā tīng wán yǐhòu, qǐng xuǎn chū zhèngquè de dá'àn.
After listening, please choose the correct answer.

0:20 好，我們開始吧。
hǎo, wǒmen kāishǐ ba.
OK, let’s start.

0:22 第一題。
dì yī tí.
Question 1.

0:24 經理，這是我這週的工作總結，請您看一下。
jīnglǐ, zhè shì wǒ zhè zhōu de gōngzuò zǒngjié, qǐng nín kàn yīxià.
Manager, this is my work summary for this week, please take a look.

0:31 他們最可能在哪兒?
tāmen zuì kěnéng zài nǎr?
Where are they most likely?

0:34 A. 醫院 B. 辦公室 C. 超市
A. yīyuàn B. bàngōngshì C. chāoshì
A. Hospital B. Office C. Supermarket

0:40 答案是B，辦公室。
dá'àn shì B, bàngōngshì.
The answer is B, office.

0:44 因為說到了經理和工作總結。
yīnwèi shuō dàole jīnglǐ hé gōngzuò zǒngjié.
Because "manager" and "work summary" were mentioned.

0:48 第二題。
dì èr tí.
Question 2.

0:50 外面下雨了，你出門的時候記得帶傘。
wàimiàn xià yǔ le, nǐ chūmèn de shíhou jìde dài sǎn.
It’s raining outside, remember to take an umbrella when you go out.

0:56 現在天氣怎麼樣?
xiànzài tiānqì zěnmeyàng?
How is the weather now?

0:59 A. 晴天 B. 陰天 C. 下雨
A. qíngtiān B. yīntiān C. xià yǔ
A. Sunny B. Cloudy C. Raining

1:05 答案是C，下雨。
dá'àn shì C, xià yǔ.
The answer is C, raining.

1:09 第三題。
dì sān tí.
Question 3.

1:11 這件衣服有點兒貴，能不能便宜一點兒?
zhè jiàn yīfu yǒudiǎnr guì, néng bù néng piányi yīdiǎnr?
This piece of clothing is a bit expensive, can it be a bit cheaper?

1:18 她想做什麼?
tā xiǎng zuò shénme?
What does she want to do?

1:21 A. 買衣服 B. 賣衣服 C. 洗衣服
A. mǎi yīfu B. mài yīfu C. xǐ yīfu
A. Buy clothes B. Sell clothes C. Wash clothes

1:27 答案是A，買衣服。
dá'àn shì A, mǎi yīfu.
The answer is A, buy clothes.

1:31 而且她在討價還價。
érqiě tā zài tǎojià huánjià.
And she is bargaining.

1:34 第四題。
dì sì tí.
Question 4.

1:36 醫生，我的頭很疼，還有點兒發燒。
yīshēng, wǒ de tóu hěn téng, hái yǒudiǎnr fāshāo.
Doctor, my head hurts a lot, and I have a bit of a fever.

1:43 他怎麼了?
tā zěnme le?
What’s wrong with him?

1:46 A. 累了 B. 生病了 C. 餓了
A. lèi le B. shēngbìng le C. è le
A. Tired B. Sick C. Hungry

1:52 答案是B，生病了。
dá'àn shì B, shēngbìng le.
The answer is B, sick.

1:56 第五題。
dì wǔ tí.
Question 5.

1:58 週末我們去爬山吧，那兒的空氣很好。
zhōumò wǒmen qù páshān ba, nàr de kōngqì hěn hǎo.
Let’s go mountain climbing this weekend, the air there is very good.

2:05 他們週末打算做什麼?
tāmen zhōumò dǎsuàn zuò shénme?
What do they plan to do this weekend?

2:08 A. 跑步 B. 游泳 C. 爬山
A. pǎobù B. yóuyǒng C. páshān
A. Running B. Swimming C. Mountain climbing

2:14 答案是C，爬山。
dá'àn shì C, páshān.
The answer is C, mountain climbing.

2:18 第六題。
dì liù tí.
Question 6.

2:20 你看，那隻貓多可愛啊!
nǐ kàn, nà zhī māo duō kě'ài a!
Look, how cute that cat is!

2:25 她覺得貓怎麼樣?
tā juéde māo zěnmeyàng?
How does she think the cat is?

2:28 A. 很漂亮 B. 很可愛 C. 很大
A. hěn piàoliang B. hěn kě'ài C. hěn dà
A. Very beautiful B. Very cute C. Very big

2:34 答案是B，很可愛。
dá'àn shì B, hěn kě'ài.
The answer is B, very cute.

2:38 第七題。
dì qī tí.
Question 7.

2:40 我想借這本書，大概什麼時候要還?
wǒ xiǎng jiè zhè běn shū, dàgài shénme shíhou yào huán?
I want to borrow this book, roughly when should I return it?

2:47 他在哪兒?
tā zài nǎr?
Where is he?

2:50 A. 圖書館 B. 銀行 C. 公園
A. túshūguǎn B. yínháng C. gōngyuán
A. Library B. Bank C. Park

2:56 答案是A，圖書館。
dá'àn shì A, túshūguǎn.
The answer is A, library.

3:00 第八題。
dì bā tí.
Question 8.

3:02 昨天的考試太難了，我有很多題都不會做。
zuótiān de kǎoshì tài nán le, wǒ yǒu hěnduō tí dōu bù huì zuò.
Yesterday’s exam was too difficult, I didn't know how to do many questions.

3:09 他覺得考試怎麼樣?
tā juéde kǎoshì zěnmeyàng?
What did he think of the exam?

3:12 A. 很簡單 B. 很有意思 C. 非常難
A. hěn jiǎndān B. hěn yǒuyìsi C. fēicháng nán
A. Very simple B. Very interesting C. Extremely difficult

3:18 答案是C，非常難。
dá'àn shì C, fēicháng nán.
The answer is C, extremely difficult.

3:22 第九題。
dì jiǔ tí.
Question 9.

3:24 咖啡太苦了，我想加點兒糖。
kāfēi tài kǔ le, wǒ xiǎng jiā diǎnr táng.
The coffee is too bitter, I want to add some sugar.

3:30 他想在咖啡裡加什麼?
tā xiǎng zài kāfēi lǐ jiā shénme?
What does he want to add to his coffee?

3:33 A. 牛奶 B. 糖 C. 水
A. niúnǎi B. táng C. shuǐ
A. Milk B. Sugar C. Water

3:39 答案是B，糖。
dá'àn shì B, táng.
The answer is B, sugar.

3:43 第十題。
dì shí tí.
Question 10.

3:45 這是我的新電話號碼，你有事可以給我打電話。
zhè shì wǒ de xīn diànhuà hàomǎ, nǐ yǒushì kěyǐ gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà.
This is my new phone number, you can call me if anything comes up.

3:52 他給了對方什麼?
tā gěile duìfāng shénme?
What did he give the other person?

3:55 A. 照片 B. 錢 C. 電話號碼
A. zhàopiàn B. qián C. diànhuà hàomǎ
A. Photo B. Money C. Phone number

4:01 答案是C，電話號碼。
dá'àn shì C, diànhuà hàomǎ.
The answer is C, phone number.

4:05 好了，今天的聽力練習就到這兒。
hǎo le, jīntiān de tīnglì liànxí jiù dào zhèr.
OK, that’s it for today's listening practice.

4:09 大家做對了幾題?
dàjiā zuò duìle jǐ tí?
How many questions did you get right?

4:11 歡迎在評論區告訴我們。
huānyíng zài pínglùn qū gàosù wǒmen.
Welcome to tell us in the comment section.

4:14 謝謝大家，下次再見。
xièxiè dàjiā, xià cì zàijiàn.
Thank you everyone, see you next time.

4:17 再見。
zàijiàn.
Goodbye.
